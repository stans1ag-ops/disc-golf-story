# Reference notes — Snow White

Style: warm watercolor storybook on lightly textured cream paper, fine pencil
and ink contours, warm muted colors, landscape composition, child-friendly.
Source text: Brothers Grimm KHM 053, Margaret Hunt translation, classic
unabridged version (all three temptations, coffin vigil, original ending).

## Snow White (`snow-white_ref.jpg`)
Young maiden: skin white as snow, cheeks and lips red as blood, long hair
black as ebony, gentle kind face, ivory wool dress with crimson velvet
cloak, wildflowers. PICTURED AS A YOUNG MAIDEN THROUGHOUT, as in most
picture-book editions; the words keep the tale's own ages (seven at the
huntsman scene, grown by the wedding). Note: the first portrait framing as
a young girl was refused by the image tool, so the maiden framing is the
approved anchor — do not re-attempt a child portrait of her.

## The Queen (`queen_ref.jpg`)
Tall, angular, coldly beautiful; high cheekbones, piercing pale eyes,
severe posture; obsidian velvet, arterial crimson silk, high-collared
regal cloak with peacock-eye embroidery, crown of sharp gilded spires.
Disguises keep her cold eyes and a glint of crimson: peddler woman
(stooped, hooded shawl), old woman (hunched, heavy headwrap), peasant
wife (earthy cloak, basket of orchard fruit).

## The Good Queen (`good-queen_ref.jpg`)
Gentle, frail, warm-featured woman with dark hair and compassionate eyes;
soft winter-white fur-trimmed gown, deep burgundy shawl; embroidery hoop,
silver needle, ebony window frame.

## The Huntsman (`huntsman_ref.jpg`)
Broad-shouldered, weather-beaten forester with a thick beard and
conflicted sorrowful eyes; moss-green leather jerkin, heavy brown wool
cloak, mud-stained boots; hunting horn, crossbow.

## The Seven Dwarfs (`dwarfs_ref.jpg`)
Compact, stocky, ancient woodland miners with long beards and distinct
expressions; earthy mineral-tone tunics (copper, slate, moss, ochre,
rust, indigo) with leather belts and peaked felt caps; brass lanterns,
iron pickaxes.

## The Prince (`prince_ref.jpg`)
Tall, earnest young nobleman with warm posture and open expression;
sapphire blue and burnished gold riding tunic, white travel cloak;
dapple-grey horse.

## The Cottage (`cottage_ref.jpg`)
Tiny thatched woodland cottage among fern-draped boulders and morning
glories, smoke from the chimney, warm light in the windows. Interior
beats (seven tiny plates, mugs, beds) keep the same honeyed wood and
cream linen.

## Scene plan (15 narrative scenes + cover)
Cover: Blood on Snow, Ebony Frame (symbolic: Snow White centre, dwarfs'
lanterns below, queen's reflection above, two-toned apple).
1 Three Drops of Blood in Midwinter — 2 The Queen and the Magic
Looking-Glass — 3 Mercy in the Deep Woods — 4 Flight Through the Seven
Mountains — 5 The Little Cottage Beyond the Hills — 6 Seven Lanterns
Round the Seventh Bed — 7 Morning Covenant at the Cottage Door — 8 The
First Temptation: The Silk Stay-Laces — 9 The Second Temptation: The
Poisoned Comb — 10 Brewing the Two-Toned Apple — 11 The Third
Temptation: The Halved Apple — 12 Vigil by the Glass Coffin — 13 The
Prince's Plea on the Mountain — 14 The Stumble on the Mountain Path —
15 The Royal Wedding & the Final Reckoning.
