# Tyson and the Tooth That Took a Bounce
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## Wiggle, wobble, not quite yet

Tyson brushed and stopped to stare;<br>
A tiny tooth was wobbling there.<br>
“My first loose tooth!” he cried with glee.<br>
“A wiggly tooth belongs to me!”

Dad, Alex, said, “Let's see it go—<br>
Just gentle wiggles, nice and slow.”<br>
Theresa smiled, her eyes so bright:<br>
“We'll let it go when time is right.”

With clean hands, Tyson gently tried;<br>
That little tooth went side to side.<br>
He nudged it with his tongue once more;<br>
It held on tighter than before.

“Wiggle, wobble! Won't you fall?”<br>
It would not budge much more at all.<br>
“Let's take a break,” he heard Dad say.<br>
So Tyson dashed outside to play.

## The bounce he didn't notice

The trampoline was round and green,<br>
The bounciest thing he'd ever seen.<br>
Its net zipped shut; Mom watched nearby,<br>
While Tyson bounced beneath the sky.

A little hop! A happy spin!<br>
A breeze tickled his cheek and chin.<br>
He laughed and bounced above the ground;<br>
His tooth slipped out without a sound.

It tumbled past the padded side<br>
And in the grass it went to hide.<br>
No tiny tap. No shout of “Hey!”<br>
He never felt it slip away.

When play was done, he touched his chin.<br>
His tongue explored his toothy grin.<br>
He found a gap! His eyes grew round:<br>
“My tooth is gone—but can't be found!”

## A note instead of a tooth

They searched the grass; they checked each shoe.<br>
They looked beneath the trampoline, too.<br>
But not a speck of tooth was there.<br>
Tyson sniffled in the evening air.

“No tooth beneath my pillow tonight!<br>
The fairy won't come on her flight!”<br>
Mom hugged him close. Dad held him tight.<br>
“Let's leave a note,” said Mom. “We'll write.”

Dear Tooth Fairy, my tooth came free<br>
While I was bouncing happily.<br>
I didn't notice it fall that day;<br>
It landed somewhere far away.

We searched and searched, but couldn't see<br>
The little tooth that came from me.<br>
Please visit even though it's gone.<br>
Love, Tyson. Then he gave a yawn.

## A dollar and a brand-new grin

He tucked the note, then climbed in bed.<br>
Mom kissed his cheek; Dad stroked his head.<br>
The moon shone softly, silver-white;<br>
His little room grew still that night.

On whisper-wings, a fairy flew<br>
And read the note that told her true.<br>
“A missing tooth? That's quite all right.<br>
Your note has brought me here tonight.”

She left a dollar, crisp and green,<br>
Beneath his pillow, quite unseen.<br>
At dawn he peeked—then gave a shout:<br>
“She came! She came! It all worked out!”

Theresa laughed; Dad grinned along.<br>
His tooth was lost, but hope stayed strong.<br>
One dollar! One new gap to show!<br>
And one more way for him to grow.

> **Say it with Tyson:** Wiggle, wobble, bounce away! A little note can save the day!
