# Reference notes — The Selfish Giant (DRAFT)

Style: Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper
with fine pencil and ink contours, warm muted colors, expressive child-friendly faces,
a Victorian fairy-tale garden, landscape composition near 16:9 for scenes.

## Characters (fixed descriptions — reuse verbatim in every scene prompt)

- Giant: a tall, broad-shouldered, gentle-faced giant of a man with a kind round face,
  short grey-brown beard, warm brown tunic, dark green cloak, and sturdy leather boots.
  (Older scenes: same man with a long grey beard.)
- Little boy: a very small child with soft golden curls, a gentle smile, and a simple
  white tunic with soft sandals.

## Settings

- Spring garden: a large lovely walled garden with soft green grass, flowers like stars,
  twelve peach-trees in delicate pink-and-pearl blossom, and a stone castle behind.
- Winter garden: the same walled garden under snow, trees frosted silver and bare, cold
  blue-white light, the stone castle behind.

## Image-safety rules for this story (hard)

- The child at the end is a beautiful child in white, nothing more: never show wound,
  nail, or mark imagery on hands or feet; keep hands soft and simple.
- The final scene shows the blossom-covered tree and the children, never a body.
- No gore, weapons, or frightening scenes. The axe and the sword in the text are NEVER
  shown in any image. The notice-board is shown from behind / blank so no lettering
  appears. No text, logos, or watermarks in any image.
- Winter personifications (Snow, Frost, Wind, Hail) are shown as weather only, never as
  menacing figures.
