One Card Left

Shane has a secret card up his sleeve in a bedtime game with Dad.

⏱️ 2-3 min read • 🎨 2 Watercolor Illustrations • 🗣️ Short lines to join in
Scene 1 • The bedtime game

Shane sat on the rug in his pajamas. Dad shuffled the Uno cards beside the fire. “One game before bed,” Dad said.

Shane looked at his hand. Red, blue, yellow, green—and one card he tucked behind the others. Dad laid down a blue card. Shane matched it. Dad played another. Back and forth the colors went.

Shane kept peeking at his hidden card. Dad noticed. “Do you have a plan?” he asked. “Maybe,” said Shane. He tried not to smile.

Scene 2 • One card left

Shane played a red card. Now he held just one. “UNO!” he called.

Dad sat up. “Only one? Then I’d better think.” He chose a green card and set it on the pile. Shane looked at the green card. Then he looked at his own. Dad leaned forward.

“Can you play?” Dad asked. Shane waited one long second. “Yes,” he said. He turned over his last card: Wild Draw Four!

🗣️ Say it with Shane

One card left!

Scene 3 • Good game

Dad stared at the card. “You saved that the whole time?” Shane nodded. Dad drew four cards, counting them aloud. “One, two, three, four.”

“I won!” Shane said. Dad gave him a high five. “You did. And you were very sneaky.”

They put the cards back in the box. The fire crackled. Shane curled up beside Dad for a moment before bed.

“Same time tomorrow?” Shane asked. “Deal,” said Dad. “But tomorrow I’m watching that secret card.”
