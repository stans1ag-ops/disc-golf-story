# Reference notes — The Ransom of Red Chief

Style: Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper
with fine pencil and ink contours, warm muted colors, expressive child-friendly faces,
rural Alabama in 1907, landscape composition near 16:9 for scenes.

## Characters (fixed descriptions — reuse verbatim in every scene prompt)

- Sam: a lean sly man in his thirties with dark hair under a worn brown felt hat,
  clean-shaven, grey work shirt, brown vest, dark trousers and boots.
- Bill Driscoll: a heavyset round-faced man in his forties with reddish-blond stubble,
  a round bowler hat, white shirt with rolled sleeves, brown suspenders and dark trousers.
- Johnny: a ten-year-old boy with bright red hair, a freckled face and a mischievous grin,
  two dark buzzard tail-feathers stuck in his hair, homespun shirt and knee breeches.
- Ebenezer Dorset: a stern elderly gentleman in his sixties with a grey beard and spectacles,
  black suit and stiff collar, calm and unflappable.

## Settings

- Cave camp: cedar-covered mountainside, cave mouth with a big rock, campfire, tin coffee
  pot, folded blankets, wooden crates.
- Summit: flat small Alabama town main street, small wooden hotel with front steps, dusty
  road, wooden storefronts, white farmhouse.
- Dorset house: modest white farmhouse front porch at night, wooden front door, lantern
  light, moonlight.

## Image-safety rules for this story

Keep everything comedic and gentle, no suffering shown. Never depict: a knife, a scalping,
a tomahawk or any weapon, or a child threatening or harming an adult. The daybreak scene
shows Bill hollow-eyed while Johnny sleeps; the sling scene shows whirling only, nobody
struck. Letter papers show no readable words.
