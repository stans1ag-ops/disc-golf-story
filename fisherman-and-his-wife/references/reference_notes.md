# Reference notes — The Fisherman and His Wife

Style: warm watercolor storybook on lightly textured cream paper, fine pencil
and ink contours, warm muted colors, landscape composition, child-friendly.

## Fisherman (`fisherman_ref.jpg`)
Elderly fisherman: kind worried face, grey beard and windswept grey hair,
weathered skin, simple worn brown tunic with rolled sleeves, straw hat in
hand, long fishing rod. Gentle and reluctant by nature.

## Wife (`wife_ref.jpg`)
Stout older wife: stern determined face, grey hair in a tight bun, plain dark
dress with a white apron, hands on hips. Base look; later scenes dress her
up (fine gown, king's robes and crown, emperor's crown and globe, pope's
golden garment and three crowns) while keeping the same face and bun.

## Flounder (`flounder_ref.jpg`)
Great enchanted flounder: large flat golden-brown fish with gentle wise
eyes, swimming just under clear green-blue water, sunlight rippling through.

## Seaside (`seaside_ref.jpg`)
Humble hovel by the sea-shore: small weathered wooden hut with a thatched
roof on a sandy shore, fishing nets and a wooden bucket outside, calm sea
beyond. The sea itself changes with each wish: clear, then green and yellow,
purple and dark blue and grey, dark grey, black and thick with foam, stormy
mountains-high waves, and at the end calm again by the old hovel.

## Scene plan (19)
1 The Hovel by the Sea — 2 The Great Flounder — 3 "Did You Wish for
Nothing?" — 4 The Green and Yellow Sea — 5 The Pretty Cottage — 6 "A Large
Stone Castle" — 7 The Purple Sea — 8 The Stone Castle — 9 "I Must Be King"
— 10 The Dark Grey Sea — 11 The Throne of Gold and Diamonds — 12 "I Must Be
Emperor" — 13 The Black Sea — 14 The Emperor's Throne — 15 "Next I Will Be
Pope" — 16 The Tempest Sea — 17 The Pope's Throne — 18 "Power Over the Sun
and Moon" — 19 Back in the Old Hovel.

Original ending kept: the last wish sends them back to the old hovel, where
they sit to this very day.
