# The Super-Duper Snowy Sledding Slide
*A Bedtime Adventure for Shane and Alex*
*Reading Time: 4-5 minutes • Rhyming & Alliterative Bedtime Story*

---

### Scene 1: Bundled-Up Astronauts Climb Mount Snowball

The world outside was a marshmallow sheet,  
Puffy and powdery, snowy and sweet!  
Every pine tree wore a hat of white,  
Dusted in crystal winter light.  
Down from the chimney the woodsmoke curled,  
On the coldest day in the whole wide world!  

Inside the hallway, zipper by zip,  
Two brave adventurers started their trip!  
Shane was all bundled in puffy bright red,  
Looking like a little astronaut instead!  
Thick winter snow-pants and heavy snow-boots,  
Striped woolen mittens and cozy warm suits!  
On top of his head perched a pom-pom hat,  
That wobbled this way, then wobbled to that!  

Dad Alex stepped out in his jacket of blue,  
With warm winter boots and a wool beanie too.  
Behind them they pulled on a yellow rope cord:  
A cherry-red sled like a thunderbolt board!  

Up, up, up the steep snowy hill,  
Where the whistling winter winds never stood still!  
Shane puffed out his breath like a steam-engine train:  
*Puff-puff-puff* on the snowy terrain!  
His cheeks were as rosy as apples in fall,  
As they conquered the top of Mount Snowball!  

"Ready to ride?" Alex asked with a grin,  
Tucking the scarf tight under Shane’s chin.  
Shane jumped in the front, shouting loud and clear,  
The sillies of sledding for all folks to hear:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Sledding Chant:**  
> *"Swoosh-a-loo and whoosh-a-lee,*  
> *Down the white hill go we!*  
> *Hold on tight and steer it straight,*  
> *Flying like a rocket past the garden gate!"*  

---

### Scene 2: The Sonic Sledding Swoosh!

Alex climbed in right behind his boy,  
Gripping the edges of red plastic joy.  
"Ready, Shane? Three, two, one... BLASTOFF!"  

With a mighty kick from Alex's boot,  
The red sled launched down the slippery chute!  
*SWOOOOOOOOOOOSH!*  

Down, down, down the powdery white,  
Flying as fast as a bird in flight!  
Snowflakes whipped in a whirlwind cloud,  
While Shane and his dad laughed long and loud!  

*BUMP-ITY-BUMP!* over a snowy hump!  
A little airborne flying jump!  
Shane squealed with pure, bubbling delight,  
Holding the rope with all of his might!  
A giant rooster-tail sprayed up behind,  
Leaving a shiny white river to wind!  

"Faster than a cheetah!" Shane shouted in glee,  
"Watch out, snowdrift! Here come we!"  
The houses and pine trees went whizzing in blur,  
As cold winter breezes made winter coats purr!  

Shane sang the refrain as they tore through the snow,  
Faster and faster the red sled would go:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Sledding Chant:**  
> *"Swoosh-a-loo and whoosh-a-lee,*  
> *Down the white hill go we!*  
> *Hold on tight and steer it straight,*  
> *Flying like a rocket past the garden gate!"*  

With a gentle *shhhhh-slide* in the powder below,  
The sled slowed to stop in a bank of soft snow!  

---

### Scene 3: Snow Angels and Hot Cocoa Moustaches

They tumbled out into the fluffy white deep,  
Where snowy-soft pillows lay quiet and asleep!  
Shane flapped his arms: *swish-swish, swoosh-swoosh!*  
Flapping his snow-legs with a merry soft push!  
Beside him, Dad Alex flapped his arms too,  
Making snow angels beneath the pale blue!  

Three perfect snow angels, smiling and neat,  
With pointy white wings from their heads to their feet!  
Shane grinned at the sky, his eyes full of spark,  
As afternoon shadows began to grow dark.  

Dad reached in his pocket and pulled out a prize:  
A stainless-steel thermos before Shane's eyes!  
He twisted the lid with a *creak* and a *pop*,  
And poured steaming cocoa right up to the top!  

From a little brown bag, with a magical wink,  
Dad dropped four fat marshmallows into the drink!  
*Plop-plop-plop-plop!* They floated on cream,  
Melting like clouds in a chocolate dream!  

Shane took a big sip from his warm paper cup...  
*SLUUUUUUUUURP!*  
When he lowered the mug and smiled straight up,  
Alex burst out with a belly-laugh roar:  
Shane had the finest white mustache in store!  
A thick, puffy marshmallow mustache of white,  
Curling right up to his dimples in sight!  

"Look at that mustache, Sir Sledder Shane!"  
Laughed Alex, hugging his boy once again.  
They sat on the bench wrapped in blanket and fleece,  
Sipping their cocoa in cozy sweet peace.  

Shane gave a warm sigh and leaned into Dad's chest,  
"That was the greatest snowy slide... and the best!"  
They sang the soft finish as stars started bright,  
Guiding the two tired sledders to night:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Sledding Chant:**  
> *"Swoosh-a-loo and whoosh-a-lee,*  
> *Tired little sledders are we!*  
> *Cocoa is finished, the stars are in sight,*  
> *Cuddle up close and whisper: Goodnight!"*
