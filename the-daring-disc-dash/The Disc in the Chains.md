# The Disc in the Chains
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## A basket in the woods

Shane held his orange Roadrunner disc at the first tee. Far down the path stood a basket with silver chains. “I want to hear them ring,” he told Dad.

“Then let’s give it a try,” said Dad. He took his pink disc from the bag. Shane looked down the fairway. One big tree stood between him and the basket.

He pointed past the trunk. “Around the tree. Toward the chains.” Dad grinned. “That’s the plan.”

> **Say it with Shane:** Around the tree. Toward the chains.

## The orange flight

Shane stepped sideways and threw. The disc sailed past the big tree, then dropped into the pine needles. A squirrel on a branch gave a little chatter.

“It made it past the tree,” said Dad. Shane ran to his disc and tried again. This time it glided right toward the basket. For one breath, Shane thought it might go in. But it landed just short.

“So close,” he said. Dad nodded. “Now you’ve got an easy shot.” Shane picked up the Roadrunner and walked closer. He could see every link in the chains.

> **Say it with Shane:** One more throw.

## Ching!

Shane held the disc with both hands. He took a breath, chose a spot in the middle of the chains, and tossed.

Ching! The disc dropped into the basket. Shane froze. Then he jumped so high Dad had to catch him.

“You heard it!” Dad said. “I made it ring!” Shane said. The squirrel chattered again, as if it had been waiting for that sound too.

Dad tapped his pink disc against Shane’s orange one. They walked to the next tee together. Shane carried the Roadrunner under his arm, already looking for the next basket.

> **Say it with Shane:** Ching!
