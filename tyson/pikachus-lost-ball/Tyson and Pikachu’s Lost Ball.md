# Tyson and Pikachu’s Lost Ball
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## A tail in the bush

Tyson and his mom, Theresa, were at the park. A yellow tail poked out of a bush. “Pika?” Tyson bent down. It was Pikachu!

“Hi!” said Tyson. “What is wrong?” Pikachu pointed up. A red ball sat in a tree. “Your ball is stuck,” said Mom.

## Shake the tree

Tyson looked at the low branch. He could not reach it. “I have a plan,” he said. “Can we shake the tree?” Mom held the trunk. Tyson held it, too. They gave it a small shake.

One leaf fell. It landed on Pikachu’s head. “Nice hat,” said Tyson.

## The pool noodle

Pikachu shook off the leaf. The ball did not move. Tyson sat on the grass to think. Then he saw a long blue tube in Mom’s bag. His pool noodle!

“Can we use that?” he asked. Mom held up the soft noodle. It just reached the ball. Tap. Tap. Nothing. “Try from this side,” said Tyson. Mom moved. Tyson stood back with Pikachu. Tap!

## Boing!

The ball fell. Boing! It hit the grass. Boing! It bounced right into Mom’s open bag. Pikachu ran to the bag. Out came the ball. Out came a sock, too. Pikachu had the sock on one ear!

Tyson giggled. “That is an even better hat.” They played catch on the grass. Tyson threw. Mom caught. Pikachu rolled the ball back. At last, it was time to go. Pikachu gave Tyson a soft hug. “Same park next time?” asked Tyson. “Pika, pika!” Tyson smiled. He was sure that meant yes.

> **Say it with Tyson:** Pika, pika!
