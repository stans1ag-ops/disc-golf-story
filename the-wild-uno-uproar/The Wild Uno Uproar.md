# The Wild Uno Uproar
*A Bedtime Adventure for Shane and Alex*
*Reading Time: 4-5 minutes • Rhyming & Alliterative Bedtime Story*

---

### Scene 1: The Master of the Bedtime Match

Outside the big window, the nighttime was still,  
The stars shone like diamonds atop the dark hill.  
Inside the warm cabin, the fire burned bright,  
Casting long dancing shadows of amber and light.  

Dressed in his dinosaur sleeper PJ’s,  
Sat five-year-old Shane with a mischievous gaze!  
Cross-legged and cozy upon the soft rug,  
Beside his dad Alex, with hot cider in mug.  

Between them lay battle! Between them lay fun!  
The ultimate showdown before day was done!  
A shiny red Uno box opened up wide,  
With one hundred eight colorful cards held inside!  

*Riffle-riffle, snap-slap!* Dad shuffled the deck,  
Shane leaned right in close, rubbernecking his neck!  
Seven bright cards dealt to Alex in line,  
And seven dealt straight to the hands of young Shane!  

Shane fanned out his cards in a rainbow display:  
Red twos, yellow skips, and blue fours in array!  
He hid them right close to his dinosaur chest,  
Squinting his eyes to look like the best!  
His eyebrows bent down in a comical frown,  
The funniest poker face in the whole town!  

Alex grinned back, "Buddy, what do you hold?  
A hand made of silver? A hand made of gold?"  
Shane giggled behind his cards, ready to play,  
And started his silly Uno chant for the day:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Uno Chant:**  
> *"Shuffle and slap and lay it neat,*  
> *Shane’s got cards that can’t be beat!*  
> *Red, yellow, green, or blue,*  
> *Draw-Four, Dad, and UNO too!"*  

---

### Scene 2: The Rainbow Thunderbolt Attack!

The first card flipped over: a shiny Blue Eight!  
"My turn!" said Dad Alex, "Let's see your sad fate!"  
Alex laid down a Blue Two with a slap,  
Shane didn't blink, he was ready to trap!  

Shane placed down a Red Two—the color flipped red!  
"Aha!" chuckled Alex, and tilted his head.  
Alex played Red Five. Shane dropped a Red Skip!  
"Hey, skipping your dad?" Alex smiled with a lip!  

Shane tapped on the rug, "You can't play, Dad! My turn!"  
He dropped a Red Reverse, making the game churn!  
Dad had to draw one: *zzzt!* from the pile!  
Shane watched his dad draw with a triumphant smile!  

Then Dad dropped a Green Seven down on the wood.  
"Can you beat that, Shane? Do you think that you could?"  
Shane paused for a moment. He looked at his hand.  
He held just two cards—the grandest in the land!  

A wicked, sweet giggle escaped from his nose,  
He wiggled his toes inside dinosaur hose!  
He took a deep breath, and he lifted high up  
A card that could rattle a porcelain cup!  

*SLAAAAAAAAAAAAAP!*  

Down on the table the thunderbolt flew:  
A **WILD DRAW FOUR**, black and rainbow in hue!  
Four colors of power, explosive and bold,  
The mightiest weapon an Uno hand can hold!  

"DRAW FOUR, DAD!" screamed Shane with a laugh and a cheer,  
The loudest sound heard in the living room here!  

Alex dropped his jaw open in theatrical grief,  
Clutching his head in complete disbelief!  
"Not the Draw Four! Oh, the sorrow! The pain!  
Defeated by five-year-old Captain Shane!"  

Shane threw his hands up and danced on the mat,  
Singing the silly chant right where he sat:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Uno Chant:**  
> *"Shuffle and slap and lay it neat,*  
> *Shane’s got cards that can’t be beat!*  
> *Red, yellow, green, or blue,*  
> *Draw-Four, Dad, and UNO too!"*  

---

### Scene 3: The Shout of Uno and the Bedtime Hug

Dad reached to the pile: *one, two, three, and four!*  
His hand was so fat it dropped down to the floor!  
"What color do you name, Master Shane of the Deck?"  
Asked Alex, laughing, scratching his neck.  

Shane pointed his finger with theatrical poise:  
"The color is YELLOW!" he shouted with noise!  

And then came the rule that every player knows well:  
Shane held just ONE card, as light as a shell.  
He raised it straight up to the ceiling so high,  
And let out a mighty and musical cry:  

"**UNO!**" yelled Shane, with a bounce and a bound,  
The happiest word that the nighttime had found!  

Dad Alex looked down at his cards with a sigh,  
He laid down a Yellow, but Shane didn't cry—  
Shane slapped his last card: a Yellow Five neat,  
Making the victory grand and complete!  

"I WON!" cheered Shane, tumbling onto the rug,  
As Alex reached out with a giant bear hug!  
He scooped Shane up tight, giving tickles and squeezes,  
While little Shane giggled as hard as he pleases!  

"You're the Uno Champion," whispered Dad in his ear,  
"The cleverest, fastest kid far and near."  
Shane nestled his head under Alex's chin,  
Worn out from the laughter and weary of win.  

The fire crackled softly with embers of red,  
Signaling two sleepy players to bed.  
Shane murmured the sleepy last line of the song,  
Where dreams and sweet adventures belong:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Uno Chant:**  
> *"Cards put away and laid down neat,*  
> *Bedtime has come on sleepy feet!*  
> *Uno was wild, but cuddle up tight,*  
> *Shane loves his Dad... and whispers: Goodnight!"*
