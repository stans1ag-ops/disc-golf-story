# The Daring Disc Golf Dash
*A Bedtime Adventure for Shane and Alex*
*Reading Time: 4-5 minutes • Rhyming & Alliterative Bedtime Story*

---

### Scene 1: The Surfer Stance on the Sunny Tee

The morning mist on maple trees,  
Was tickled by a buzzing breeze.  
Through Whispering Woods so fresh and clean,  
Walked Alex and Shane through shades of green.  
Two daring disc golf dynamos,  
From sturdy caps to sneaker toes!  

In Alex’s bag of zipper pride,  
A bubblegum pink disc slept inside.  
And Shane clutched tight his favorite toy:  
A fiery orange disc of joy!  
The **Roadrunner**, blazing bright,  
Eager to take its zooming flight!  

On Tee Pad One, Dad stopped to show  
How daring disc golf dynamos throw.  
"Stand like a surfer on the sea,  
Bend your knees and set them free!  
Pull it straight across your chest,  
And snap your wrist to do your best!"  

Shane gripped the rim with fingers tight,  
His twinkling eyes were glowing bright.  
He took a hop, he turned his toe,  
And let the silly chorus flow:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Disc Chant:**  
> *"Zippity-zap and flippity-flop,*  
> *This zooming disc will never stop!*  
> *Through the tall trees and around the bend,*  
> *Chains go CHING at the journey's end!"*  

---

### Scene 2: The Flying Orange Saucer

*SWOOOOOOSH!*  

Shane whipped his arm with speedy grace,  
A beaming smile across his face!  
The orange Roadrunner tore through air,  
Like a fiery comet spinning there!  
It sailed above the clover sweet,  
Above the dirt where pine trees meet.  

A chatty squirrel on a branch looked down,  
With bushy tail and furry crown.  
*Chitter-chatter, squeak and peep!*  
The disc did a looping figure-eight leap!  
It curved to the left, it glided right,  
Diving through beams of amber light!  

"Look at it soar!" cheered happy Shane,  
"Faster than any rocket train!"  
Alex clapped with proud delight,  
"You launched that saucer out of sight!"  

Down through the fairway, sleek and clean,  
Past giant trunks of mossy green,  
The orange flyer floated down,  
Landing softly on pine-needle ground.  

Shane skipped along with joyous pace,  
Singing the rhyme to win the race:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Disc Chant:**  
> *"Zippity-zap and flippity-flop,*  
> *This zooming disc will never stop!*  
> *Through the tall trees and around the bend,*  
> *Chains go CHING at the journey's end!"*  

---

### Scene 3: The Clattering Chime of the Chains!

Now came the basket, round and tall,  
A silver fortress for the ball...  
Or rather, the disc that Shane held near,  
As Hole Number Nine drew shining clear!  

The metal chains hung cold and bright,  
Catching the glowing woodland light.  
Alex stepped back and gave a nod:  
"You’ve got this, buddy. Trust the rod—  
I mean, the throw, the snap, the spin!  
One gentle toss and pop it in!"  

Shane aimed his eye, took in the view,  
One little breath, and away it flew!  
A gentle flutter, a spinning glide,  
Right at the metal basket side...  

*CLINK-CHING! RATTLE-RATTLE-CLANG!*  

The happiest bells in the forest rang!  
The orange Roadrunner hit the net,  
The finest ace they'd witnessed yet!  

"HOLE IN ONE!" Shane gave a leap!  
The forest critters gathered deep—  
Hedgehogs, bunnies, birds in flight,  
Chirping and cheering in the light!  

Alex scooped Shane up in the air,  
A giant bear hug right then and there!  
"An ace, my buddy! Pure and true!  
No disc golfer throws like you!"  

Shane hugged his dad and clutched his disc,  
No greater prize, no sweeter brisk.  
They tapped the pink disc to the orange flame,  
And sang the finish to their game:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Disc Chant:**  
> *"Zippity-zap and flippity-flop,*  
> *Shane's magical disc came to a stop!*  
> *Chains went CHING in the basket sweet,*  
> *The Disc Golf Champs cannot be beat!"*
