The Three Billy-Goats Gruff

Three clever goats outsmart a grumpy troll guarding the bridge to reach the sweet green meadow grass.

⏱️ 4-5 min read • 🎨 12 Story Illustrations • 🎙️ Neural Voice Narration • 🎬 Story Video

Scene 1 • Across the Valley

Once upon a time, there were three billy goats who lived down in a quiet valley. Their family name was "Gruff." Across the valley, high upon a green mountain hillside, grew sweet clover and tender meadow grass. The three goats looked up from the valley and longed to climb the hillside to feast and grow fat.

Scene 2 • The Rushing Stream

To reach that sunny hillside, they had to cross a rushing mountain stream that tumbled over rocks and stones. And right across the water stretched a narrow wooden bridge made of heavy timber planks.

Scene 3 • The Troll Beneath the Bridge

Now, under that very bridge lived a great, ugly troll! He had eyes as round and big as saucers, a long crooked nose like a poker, and messy moss in his hair. He was always grumpy, and he guarded his bridge so that no one could pass.

Scene 4 • The Smallest Goat Steps Out

Early the next morning, the smallest Billy Goat Gruff set off down the path. He had tiny hooves, soft cream-colored fur, and little button horns. Even though he was the littlest, he trotted along with a brave and cheerful heart.

Scene 5 • Trip, Trap, Trip, Trap!

He stepped right onto the wooden bridge. His tiny hooves tapped lightly against the boards.

🪵 The Wooden Bridge
Trip, trap! Trip, trap!

went the bridge under his little feet.

Scene 6 • The Troll's Roar

Suddenly, a terrible roar boomed from beneath the timber planks! The ugly troll poked his head over the rail, his saucer eyes wide with anger.

🧌 The Grumpy Troll
"WHO'S THAT TRIPPING OVER MY BRIDGE?"
Scene 7 • The Smallest Goat's Clever Escape

"Oh, it is only I, the tiniest Billy Goat Gruff," said the little goat in his small, squeaky voice. "I am going up to the hillside to make myself fat."

🧌 The Troll
"Now I am coming to gobble you up!"

"Oh, please don't take me! I am far too little," said the clever goat. "Wait a moment for the second Billy Goat Gruff. He is much bigger than I am!"

"Well, be off with you then!" grunted the greedy troll, thinking of a bigger meal. And the smallest goat skipped safely across to the meadow.

Scene 8 • The Second Goat Approaches

A little while later, down came the second Billy Goat Gruff. He was medium-sized, with a warm brown coat, sturdy legs, and curved horns. He trotted down toward the stream, eager to join his brother.

Scene 9 • Trip, Trap on the Planks

The second goat trotted onto the bridge. His hooves made a firmer, louder click on the wood.

🪵 The Wooden Bridge
Trip, trap, trip, trap, trip, trap!

went the bridge under his steady stride.

Scene 10 • The Second Challenge

Up popped the troll's hairy head once more!

🧌 The Troll
"WHO'S THAT TRIPPING OVER MY BRIDGE?"

"It is I, the second Billy Goat Gruff," said the goat in a clear, bold voice. "I am going up to the hillside to make myself fat."

"Now I am coming to gobble you up!" howled the troll.

"Oh no, do not take me!" said the second goat calmly. "Wait a little while for Big Billy Goat Gruff. He is much, much bigger than I!"

"Very well! Be off with you!" growled the greedy troll. And the second goat bounded safely across to the grassy hillside.

Scene 11 • Big Billy Goat Gruff Arrives

Just then, a deep shadow fell across the forest path. Down came the great, magnificent Big Billy Goat Gruff! He was enormous and strong, with thick shaggy grey fur, a long wispy beard, and two great curved horns as hard as stone.

He strode onto the bridge, and the heavy timbers creaked and groaned under his mighty weight:

🪵 The Bridge Creaks and Groans
TRIP, TRAP, TRIP, TRAP, TRIP, TRAP!
🧌 The Troll
"WHO'S THAT TRAMPING OVER MY BRIDGE?"
🐐 Big Billy Goat Gruff
"IT IS I! THE BIG BILLY GOAT GRUFF!"

"Now I am coming to gobble you up!" cried the troll, leaping forward onto the boards.

"Well, come along then!" shouted Big Billy Goat Gruff. He lowered his mighty horns, charged right at the bully, and with a tremendous crack and a great heave, butted the troll right off the bridge and down into the rushing river! Splash! Away the soggy troll tumbled down the cascade, never to bother anyone again!

Scene 12 • Feasting on the Hillside

Then Big Billy Goat Gruff strode triumphantly across the bridge to join his two brothers.

Up on the sunny hillside, the three Billy Goats Gruff ate the sweet green clover and tender grass all summer long. They grew so fat and round they could scarcely walk home again! And if the fat hasn't fallen off them, why, they are still fat to this very day!
