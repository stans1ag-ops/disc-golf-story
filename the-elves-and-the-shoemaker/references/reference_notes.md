# The Elves and the Shoemaker — Reference Notes

Source: "The Elves and the Shoemaker" (First Story), the Brothers Grimm, public domain.
Source URL: https://americanliterature.com/author/the-brothers-grimm/short-story/the-elves-and-the-shoemaker
Adaptation: the famous first story only; the elves wear simple ragged tunics (the original has them naked) — noted on the page.

## Fixed character descriptions (reuse verbatim in every scene prompt)

SHOEMAKER: Elderly man in his seventies, kind wrinkled face, short neat white beard, warm brown eyes, bald head with a fringe of white hair. He wears a cream linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves, a brown leather cobbler's apron, dark brown trousers, and simple worn shoes.

WIFE: Elderly woman in her sixties, gentle smiling face, rosy cheeks, grey hair in a neat bun. She wears a dark green shawl over a cream blouse, a long brown skirt, and a white apron.

ELVES: Two tiny cheerful men about a foot tall, impish smiling faces, large pointy ears, big bright eyes, small upturned noses, barefoot, wearing simple ragged beige under-tunics. One has a tuft of reddish-brown hair; the other a tuft of sandy-blond hair.

WORKSHOP: A cozy 19th-century European cobbler's workshop: wooden workbench with cobbler's tools (hammer, awl, wooden shoe lasts), rolls of leather on shelves, cut leather pieces, wooden stools, a small window with warm light, a candle on the bench, stone walls.

## Scene plan (15 beats)

1. elves_shoemaker_01 — The last piece of leather: shoemaker at dusk with one small piece of leather.
2. elves_shoemaker_02 — Cutting by candlelight: shoemaker cuts the leather pieces in the evening.
3. elves_shoemaker_03 — A clear conscience: shoemaker asleep in bed, moonlight through window.
4. elves_shoemaker_04 — Morning wonder: finished shoes on the table, shoemaker astounded.
5. elves_shoemaker_05 — A masterpiece: close-up of the perfect shoes, not one bad stitch.
6. elves_shoemaker_06 — The generous buyer: customer pays more than the usual price.
7. elves_shoemaker_07 — Leather for two pairs: shoemaker returns with new leather, cuts pieces at night.
8. elves_shoemaker_08 — Two pairs by morning: two finished pairs on the table.
9. elves_shoemaker_09 — Honest independence: prosperous workshop, four pairs, shelves full.
10. elves_shoemaker_10 — The Christmas Eve plan: shoemaker suggests to his wife they stay up and watch.
11. elves_shoemaker_11 — Hiding and watching: couple hidden behind hanging clothes with a candle.
12. elves_shoemaker_12 — The midnight workers: two elves stitching, sewing, hammering at the table.
13. elves_shoemaker_13 — A grateful plan: wife sews tiny clothes, shoemaker makes tiny shoes.
14. elves_shoemaker_14 — Gifts on the table: tiny outfits laid out instead of cut leather.
15. elves_shoemaker_15 — Dance of joy: elves dressed in fine clothes, dancing out the door singing.
