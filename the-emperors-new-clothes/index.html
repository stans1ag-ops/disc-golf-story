The Emperor's New Clothes

By Hans Christian Andersen • A cozy bedtime tale about honesty, vanity, and the courage of an innocent child.

Scene 1 • The Emperor's Wardrobe

Many years ago, in a bustling kingdom across the sea, lived an Emperor who cared more about new clothes than anything else in the whole wide world! While other rulers spent their mornings reviewing maps, inspecting castles, or meeting with ministers, the people of this city always said:

👑 The Townsfolk
“The Emperor is in his wardrobe!”

He had a different splendid coat for every single hour of the day.

Scene 2 • The Two Clever Strangers

One bright morning, two sneaky strangers came walking into the royal capital. They bowed low before the guards and announced themselves as master weavers from a distant land.

🧵 The Two Weavers
“We can weave the most magical fabric in all the world—so fine that it is completely invisible to anyone who is foolish or unfit for their job!”

They claimed they could weave the most extraordinary fabric ever known, cloth so magical that it was completely invisible to anyone who was foolish or unfit for their job!

Scene 3 • A Royal Commission

When the Emperor heard the news, his eyes danced with joy.

👑 The Emperor
“What a magnificent suit that would be! If I wore such clothes, I could discover at once which men in my kingdom are wise and which are simpletons!”

Without delay, he gave the two weavers a great chest overflowing with shiny gold coins so they could start weaving immediately.

Scene 4 • The Empty Looms

The two rogues promptly set up two large wooden looms in a grand palace hall. They demanded the finest silk thread and pure spools of spun gold. But as soon as the treasures were brought, they stuffed them straight into their own traveling knapsacks!

🧵 The Weaving Hall
Click-clack, click-clack went the empty wooden shuttles late into the night!

Then, sitting at the empty frames, they pumped the pedals and clicked their empty shuttles through the air late into the night.

Scene 5 • The Faithful Old Minister's Visit

After a few days, the Emperor grew impatient to see how his wonderful cloth was turning out. "I will send my faithful old minister," thought the Emperor. "He is sensible and wise, and nobody is better suited for his post."

The old minister adjusted his spectacles and stepped into the weaving hall. But when he looked at the looms, he stopped dead in his tracks!

Scene 6 • “Oh, How Magnificent!”

The minister rubbed his spectacles and stared again, but there was not a single thread to be seen! "Mercy on me!" thought the poor old man. "Can it be that I am foolish? No one must ever know!"

🧵 The Weavers
“Do you not admire these rich scarlet swirls, Sir Minister? Look at these golden ribbons!”
📜 The Faithful Minister
“Oh, magnificent! Exquisite colors! I shall tell the Emperor at once!”
Scene 7 • The Royal Chamberlain's Turn

Next, the Emperor sent his trusted Royal Chamberlain to find out when the suit would be finished. The Chamberlain leaned over the wooden frame, squinting left and right. Just like the minister, he saw nothing but bare wood and empty air!

🧐 The Royal Chamberlain
“Charming! A masterpiece of silk and gold! The colors are absolutely divine!”
Scene 8 • The Emperor Inspects the Looms

Now the entire city could talk of nothing else! At last, the Emperor decided to see the magical cloth for himself. Flanked by his finest courtiers, he swept into the weaving room. The weavers worked faster than ever, slicing the air with clicking scissors. The Emperor blinked once, blinked twice, and rubbed his eyes. He could not see a single strand!

👑 The Emperor's Secret Panic
“Good gracious! Can it be that I am foolish? Am I not fit to be Emperor?”
Scene 9 • The Midnight Stitching

"This is terrible!" thought the Emperor. "Am I foolish? Am I unfit to be Emperor?" But on no account would he let anyone suspect! "Oh, it has my highest royal approval!" he declared with a beaming smile.

The night before the great city procession, the weavers lit sixteen bright candles. Everyone could see them busily cutting the empty air and sewing with threadless needles.

Scene 10 • Dressing the Emperor

At dawn, the rogues raised their arms as if holding up a delicate garment.

🧵 The Weavers
“Here are your Majesty's trousers! Here is the waistcoat! And here is the royal mantle! The fabric is as light as a cobweb—you might think you have nothing on at all, but that is its greatest virtue!”

The Emperor removed his robes and prepared to try on his extraordinary new suit.

Scene 11 • Admiring the Reflection

The rogues pretended to fasten invisible buttons, smooth invisible sleeves, and drape a splendid invisible train. The Emperor twirled around slowly before a tall golden mirror, standing in his ruffled bedtime drawers and undershirt.

👑 The Courtiers
“How delightfully they fit! What a glorious pattern! What royal colors!”
Scene 12 • The Velvet Train

"The royal canopy is ready, your Majesty!" announced the master of ceremonies. The noble lords of the bedchamber knelt down upon the velvet carpet and reached out with both hands, pretending to lift the magnificent invisible train.

They marched forward with their arms held high in the air, terrified that someone might think them foolish if they let go!

Scene 13 • The Grand Procession

Under a high silk canopy embroidered with gold, the Emperor strode through the sunlit capital. The cobblestone avenue was lined with cheering crowds, and spectators leaned out of every flower-decked window.

🎉 The Cheering City
“Look at his Majesty's new clothes! What a graceful train! What a perfect fit!”

No one dared admit they could see nothing at all.

Scene 14 • The Child Speaks the Truth

Just as the procession entered the bustling town square, a little boy perched upon his father's shoulders pointed with a bright giggle.

👦 The Honest Little Boy
“Look, Papa! The Emperor has no clothes on at all!”

The father gasped, and the words whispered like a wave from neighbor to neighbor: "The child says he has nothing on!"

Scene 15 • Truth, Laughter, and a Royal March
😄 The Whole Town
“He has nothing on at all!”

The Emperor shivered, and his cheeks turned red as a ripe strawberry. He knew deep down that the people were right! But he squared his shoulders, lifted his golden crown, and marched even taller, learning that true dignity comes from honesty, not from fancy clothes.

💭 Bedtime Chat

What did the little boy teach the Emperor and his kingdom?

🌟 Courage & Honesty Even when everyone else is afraid to look silly, speaking the simple truth with a kind heart is always the bravest thing to do!
👑 True Worth Within Fancy clothes and shiny crowns don't make a good leader—kindness, honesty, and caring for others are what truly matter.
