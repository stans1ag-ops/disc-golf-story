# The Boy Who Cried Wolf — Reference Notes (approved appearance anchors)

## The Shepherd Boy (`boy_ref.jpg`)
A shepherd boy about nine: tousled dark hair, bright mischievous brown eyes, sun-tanned freckled cheeks, simple rustic tunic, brown vest, cloth belt with a shepherd's pipe tucked in it, sandals, wooden shepherd's crook. Playful, cheeky expression.

## The Wolf (`wolf_ref.jpg`)
A lean grey wolf in a cautious stalking pose: thick grey fur, amber eyes, tail low. Menacing but child-safe — never gory, no attack shown.

## The Hillside Pasture (`pasture_ref.jpg`)
A sunny hillside pasture with a flock of fluffy white sheep grazing, a winding path down to a small storybook village (cottages, church steeple) in the valley below, dark forest at the pasture's edge. Peaceful and idyllic.

## The Wise Old Man (`old_man_ref.jpg`)
A kind wise old man of the village: weathered face, white beard, kind wrinkled eyes, simple rustic cloak and tunic, walking stick. Gentle, comforting expression.

## Style
Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper; fine pencil and ink contours; warm muted colors (warm ochre, cream, muted green, brown, soft blue); expressive child-friendly faces. No text, logos, watermarks, collage panels, or photorealism.
