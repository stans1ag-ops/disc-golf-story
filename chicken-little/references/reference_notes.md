# Chicken Little — Reference Notes (approved appearance anchors)

## Henny-penny (`henny_penny_ref.jpg`)
Small cheerful reddish-brown hen. Round plump body in warm chestnut-brown; soft golden-brown wing feathers; bright red comb and wattle; curious amber eyes; pale yellow beak and legs. Expressive, slightly worried when panicked.

## Foxy-woxy (`foxy_woxy_ref.jpg`)
Sly red fox. Rust-red fur with cream chest ruff and cream cheek ruffs; big bushy tail with a white tip; pointed ears; clever narrowed amber eyes; cunning sideways smile. Mischievous, never frightening.

## The flock (`flock_ref.jpg`)
- **Cocky-locky**: proud golden-red rooster; upright scarlet comb; iridescent green-black tail feathers arched high; bold chest-out stance; yellow beak and legs.
- **Ducky-daddles**: small chubby soft-yellow duck; round sweet face; orange beak and orange webbed feet.
- **Goosey-poosey**: elegant white-grey goose; long curved neck; calm gentle dark eye; orange beak and feet.
- **Turkey-lurkey**: large round brown turkey; fanned tail with bronze-tipped feathers; blue-red wattle and snood; pinkish legs.

## Rickyard setting (`rickyard_ref.jpg`)
Cozy sunny farm rickyard: big spreading oak tree with visible acorns; golden corn scattered on warm earth; wooden fence; red barn in the soft distance; winding country lane into gentle green hills. Warm morning light.

## Style
Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper; fine pencil and ink contours; warm muted colors (warm ochre, cream, muted green, brown, soft blue); expressive child-friendly faces. No text, logos, watermarks, collage panels, or photorealism.
