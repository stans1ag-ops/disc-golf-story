# Big Walter
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## Morning at the creek

Shane and Dad reached Miller’s Creek just as the sun came up. Shane wore his orange life jacket and carried his yellow fishing rod. Dad carried the tackle box.

“Do you think Big Walter is awake?” Shane asked. Dad had told him about the old catfish in the creek. “Maybe,” Dad said. “Let’s ask him.” They sat together on the dock and cast into the quiet water.

## The bobber dives

A turtle rested on a log. Dragonflies darted over the creek. Shane’s red and white bobber barely moved.

Then it vanished. The yellow rod bent toward the water. “Dad!” Shane cried. Dad moved beside him and held the rod too. “I’ve got you,” he said. They pulled together, one slow turn of the reel at a time.

## Who is down there?

A wide gray head rose from the water. Long whiskers spread out from its mouth. Shane stared. “That’s Big Walter!”

The catfish gave one heavy swish of its tail. Dad kept the line steady while Shane reeled. At last they guided Walter into the shallows beside the dock.

“He’s bigger than my backpack,” Shane said. Dad laughed. “A lot bigger.”

## A look up close

Dad laid Walter for a moment on the damp grass beside the creek and kept a hand on him. Shane leaned in. The catfish was smooth and gray, with long whiskers by his mouth.

“Can I take a picture?” Shane asked. Dad handed him the phone. Shane took one quick photo.

“What should we do now?” Dad asked. Shane looked at the creek. “Let him go home,” he said. Together they slid Walter back into the water.

## Walter goes home

For a moment Walter stayed beside them. Then he flicked his tail. Splash! Cold creek water spotted Shane’s life jacket and Dad’s shirt.

They laughed as the big shape disappeared below the dock. Shane waved at the water. “Bye, Walter.”

On the walk home, Shane carried the yellow rod. Dad carried the tackle box. Both of them were still a little wet, and both knew exactly what story they would tell at breakfast.
