The Bouncing Bicycle Brigade

A rhyming, bell-ringing biking quest through splashy puddles and up the Big Bumpy Hill!

⏱️ 4-5 min read • 🎨 3 Watercolor Illustrations • 🗣️ Silly Rhyming Refrains
Scene 1 • Helmets Click and Wheels Roll

The driveway baked in afternoon gold, where two brave cyclists, proud and bold, checked tires and pedals, brakes and chains, ready to race down winding lanes!

Alex knelt down on a bend of his knee, as gentle and patient as dads can be. He snapped the blue helmet under Shane’s chin—CLICK! went the buckle with cheerful grin! "Safety first!" Dad gave a wink, "Faster than lightning, quicker than think!"

Shane straddled his bike of red and blue, with shiny chrome spokes all shiny and new. On his handlebar perched a silver bell, a musical wonder that rang so well! Shane gave the bell a test-drive PING! making the backyard robins sing!

"Captain Shane is ready to ride! Let's pedal, Dad, by the park trail side!" He placed his sneakers on pedals flat, adjusted his cap and began to chat:

🗣️ Sing the Bicycle Brigade Chant!

"Clickety-clack and pedal ahead,
Where rabbits run and robins tread!
Ring the bell with a ding-ding-dong,
Shane and Dad are twenty feet strong!"

Scene 2 • The Splashing Puddle of Puddle-Ville

Off went the Bouncing Bicycle Brigade! Rolling beneath the maple shade! Whirrrr-whirrrr-whirrrr went the rubber wheels, kicking up dust and gravel peels! Shane pedaled fast with his little knees, racing the butterflies through the breeze.

Around the corner and down the street, lay a giant puddle, wide and neat! Leftover rain from yesterday's storm, shining like glass in the sunshine warm! "Steer around it?" Dad called out, with a teasing twinkle and playful shout. "No way, Dad!" Shane laughed with glee, "Puddle-ville is the place for me!"

SPLOOOOOOOOOSH!

A rooster-tail spray of water high splashed in the air toward the sunny sky! Droplets flew like crystal beads, soaking the grass and the roadside weeds! Shane cheered as water splashed his shins, showing the biggest of five-year-old grins!

Dad Alex laughed and rang his bell, singing the song that they loved so well:

🗣️ Ring Your Imaginary Bell!

"Clickety-clack and pedal ahead,
Where rabbits run and robins tread!
Ring the bell with a ding-ding-dong,
Shane and Dad are twenty feet strong!"

Scene 3 • The Crest of the Big Bumpy Hill

Ahead rose the challenge, mighty and steep: the Big Bumpy Hill where the shadows sleep! A grassy green mountain that touched the sky, where neighborhood kites would often fly. "Can we climb it?" Alex asked with a grin, "Or will the Big Bumpy Mountain win?"

"We can do it!" Shane proudly said, pedaling forward, head tilted ahead! Push with the left! Push with the right! Spinning those pedals with tiger might! Shane stood on his pedals, gritting his teeth, leaving the valley far underneath!

Alex cheered, "Keep going, Shane! You're as powerful as a freight-train engine!" One final push... one final heave... they rolled to the very top crest of the hill!

DING-DONG! DING-DONG!

Below them the town was glowing bright, bathed in the golden sunset light. Shane parked his bicycle, stood up tall, feeling as big as a palace wall! He raised his hand and struck the bell: DING-DONG! DING-DONG! it rang to tell!

"We made it to the top!" Shane cried, overflowing with happy pride! Alex wrapped Shane in a sunset hug, two happy bikers, safe and snug. Together they shouted the victory song:

🏆 Mountain Summit Song!

"Clickety-clack and home we go,
Down the hill in the sunset glow!
Rang the bell with a ding-ding-dong,
Shane and Dad conquered the mountain strong!"

And coasting gently down the long smooth path, two happy cyclists pedaled home for a warm bedtime bath.
