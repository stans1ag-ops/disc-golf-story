# The Gingerbread Man

A freshly baked cookie, a very silly chase, and one hungry little boy!

## A Warm, Spicy Kitchen

One sunny morning, the cook went into the kitchen to make gingerbread. She stirred flour, water, dark treacle, ginger, and a pinch of spice in a big bowl. Round and round went her spoon. Soon the kitchen smelled sweet and warm.

She rolled the golden dough across the table. With her tins, she cut round cakes and square cakes. Then she smiled. "I shall make something special for little Bobby," she said. "A little gingerbread man!"

## A Very Jolly Little Man

The cook shaped a body, a head, two arms, and two little legs. She gave him tiny hands and feet. She made bright eyes from seeds and almonds, and a merry mouth from a raisin. On top of his head, she added a frilly sugar hat.

What a jolly little fellow he was! He almost seemed to be smiling at a secret joke. The cook put him at the back of the baking tray, behind the round cakes and the square cakes. Then she slid the tray into the oven and went upstairs to sweep.

## Hop, Skip, and Jump!

When the clock struck twelve, the cook hurried back to the kitchen. She opened the oven door. The cakes were brown and ready. So was the gingerbread man—but he was standing up!

Before the cook could blink, he hopped over the round cakes, skipped over the square cakes, and jumped past her arm. Down he landed on the kitchen floor. His tiny feet went pit-pat, pit-pat, toward the open back door.

"Come back!" called the cook. But the little fellow only sang:

> Run, run, as fast as you can! You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!

## Mouser Wakes Up

Right across the doorway lay Mouser the cat, fast asleep in the sunshine. "Mouser! Mouser! Stop the gingerbread man!" cried the cook. "I made him for little Bobby!"

Mouser rolled over. Was somebody calling in her dream? The cook called again, and the cat sprang to her feet. But just as Mouser turned her head, the gingerbread man slipped under her tail and dashed into the garden.

Now the cat ran after the cookie, and the cook ran after the cat. Down the sunny path they went, past flowers and buzzing bees.

## Between Towser's Legs

At the bottom of the garden, Towser the dog was snoozing beside a warm stone wall. "Towser! Towser! Stop him!" called the cook. The dog gave a snore. She called again. At last he jumped up to see what the fuss was about.

That was the gingerbread man's chance. He darted between the dog's legs and scrambled onto the wall. Towser looked down the path and saw Mouser running toward him, with the cook close behind. Where was that little cookie?

From the top of the wall came a cheerful song:

> Run, run, as fast as you can! You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!

## What a Tumble!

Towser thought the cook wanted him to catch the cat. Off he rushed toward Mouser! Mouser was running so fast that she could not stop. Bump! The cat and dog rolled into a noisy tangle.

The cook tried to stop too. Her apron fluttered. Her feet slipped. Over she went, right into the middle of the muddle!

There were barks and meows and a great deal of scrambling. A dog paw appeared here. A cat tail appeared there. Then the cook's head popped up between them. What a tumble they had taken!

## Laughing on the Wall

Up on the wall, the gingerbread man laughed until his little seed eyes shone. Nobody had caught him. He was sure nobody ever could!

Mouser untangled herself and went back to the kitchen. She had had quite enough of chasing cookies. Towser shook himself, looked up, and finally spotted the little fellow. The cook stood up, straightened her apron, and followed him.

Here came the dog again! The gingerbread man turned, jumped down on the far side of the wall, and raced into the field.

> Run, run, as fast as you can! You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!

## Jocko's Great Big Leap

In the middle of the field stood a tall tree. At its foot sat Jocko the monkey. Jocko was wide awake. He saw a tiny brown fellow running through the grass, with a dog and a cook behind him.

"Jocko! Jocko! Stop the gingerbread man!" called the cook. The monkey sprang into the air. But his leap was much too big! He sailed right over the gingerbread man and landed on Towser's back.

Towser spun around in surprise. Jocko jumped away, chattering loudly. While they sorted themselves out, the gingerbread man reached the tree.

## Up, Up, Into the Tree

"A dog cannot climb a tree," thought the gingerbread man. "And I don't think the cook can climb one either. I have never met a monkey before, so I am not sure about him. But up I go!"

Hand over hand, he climbed the trunk. Higher and higher he went, until he reached a leafy branch near the top. The cook and Towser looked very small below him.

He swung one little leg over the branch and called down:

> Climb, climb, as fast as you can! You can't catch me, I'm the Gingerbread Man!

## A Monkey Can Climb!

But Jocko could climb a tree. Oh, how he could climb! In one spring he reached a low branch. In another moment he was up among the leaves.

The gingerbread man hurried to the very end of his branch. He hung there by one little hand. Jocko swung underneath, stretched out his long arm, and caught him.

Now the monkey held the little cookie up and looked at him hungrily. The gingerbread man's merry raisin smile drooped. His seed eyes filled with tears. For the first time all day, he had nothing to sing.

## Little Bobby Comes Running

Just then, little Bobby came running across the field. He had been taking a nap upstairs. In his dreams, he kept hearing somebody call his name. When he woke, he heard barking and chattering outside.

He ran downstairs without even stopping to put on his shoes. His bare feet pattered down the garden path. Over the low wall he climbed, and across the grass he ran.

Under the tree, Bobby looked up. "Drop it, Jocko!" he called. Jocko knew he must mind Bobby. He let go, and the gingerbread man fell straight into the front of Bobby's pinafore.

## Three Bites, and All Gone

Bobby lifted up the gingerbread man. What a lovely cookie! The raisin mouth drooped lower, but Bobby was hungry. He took one big bite and ate the little legs and part of the body.

"Oh!" cried the gingerbread man. "I'm one-third gone!"

Bobby took a second bite. This time the arms and the rest of the body disappeared. "Oh!" cried the gingerbread man. "I'm two-thirds gone!"

Then Bobby took a third bite and ate the head, sugar hat and all. "Oh!" said the gingerbread man. "I'm all gone!"

And so he was. The cook had found Bobby's treat at last. The chase was over, and that was the end of the gingerbread man.
