# Sleeping Beauty — reference notes

Fixed appearance anchors. Reuse this exact text in every scene prompt.

## Princess Rosamond (princess_ref.jpg)
Young princess, long flowing golden hair, soft kind face with rosy cheeks, rose-pink gown with soft gold trim, small rose circlet in her hair, pink slippers.

## The thirteenth wise woman (wise_woman_ref.jpg)
Tall proud older woman, long dark grey hair, stern severe face, deep purple-black hooded cloak with silver moon-and-star trim, dark grey gown beneath.

## The prince (prince_ref.jpg)
Young prince, warm brown wavy hair, kind brave face, deep blue tunic with gold trim, brown boots, simple gold circlet.

## The castle (castle_ref.jpg)
Fairytale castle, warm stone walls, tall round tower with conical slate-blue roof and small golden vane, smaller turrets, rose gardens and green trees, rolling green hills behind.
