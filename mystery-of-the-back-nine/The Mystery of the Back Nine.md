# The Mystery of the Back Nine
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## The first clue

At hole ten, Shane’s orange Roadrunner landed beside a pile of leaves. As he reached for it, he spotted a feather almost as long as his arm.

“Who dropped this?” he asked. Dad turned it over. Brown and black stripes ran across it. “A big bird,” Dad said. Shane slipped the feather into his bag. “Then we have a mystery.”

## Muddy tracks

They followed the path to a creek. In the soft mud were three-toed prints. Some were big. Some were tiny.

“Dinosaur feet?” Shane whispered. Dad crouched beside him. “Maybe bird feet. Look at the little ones.” Shane imagined a big bird walking with its babies. “Let’s see where they went.”

## A sound in the leaves

At hole twelve, Shane threw past a fallen log. Before he could fetch his disc, something scratched in the leaves.

Scratch, scratch. Cluck.

Shane held Dad’s hand. They moved quietly to the log and peeked over. Something brown and round moved in a patch of sunlight. Then four smaller shapes hurried after it.

## The family

It was a family of wild turkeys. One big turkey spread its tail like a fan. Another scratched the ground. Four fluffy chicks followed close behind.

One chick pecked the edge of Shane’s orange disc. Tock, tock. Shane covered his mouth so he would not laugh too loudly.

The big turkey called, “Gobble, gobble!” The chicks hurried away with their parents. Shane waited until they were gone before picking up his disc.

## Mystery solved

Shane held up the feather. Its stripes matched the big turkey’s tail. He looked back at the three-toed tracks. “It was them!” he said.

Dad tapped his pink disc against Shane’s Roadrunner. “Good looking, detective.” Shane put the feather safely in his bag. They walked to the next hole, watching the leaves in case another clue appeared.
