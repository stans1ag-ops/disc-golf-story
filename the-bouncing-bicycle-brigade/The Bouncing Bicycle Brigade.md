# The Bouncing Bicycle Brigade
*A Bedtime Adventure for Shane and Alex*
*Reading Time: 4-5 minutes • Rhyming & Alliterative Bedtime Story*

---

### Scene 1: Helmets Click and Wheels Roll!

The driveway baked in afternoon gold,  
Where two brave cyclists, proud and bold,  
Checked tires and pedals, brakes and chains,  
Ready to race down winding lanes!  

Alex knelt down on a bend of his knee,  
As gentle and patient as dads can be.  
He snapped the blue helmet under Shane’s chin—  
*CLICK!* went the buckle with cheerful grin!  

"Safety first!" Dad gave a wink,  
"Faster than lightning, quicker than think!"  
Shane straddled his bike of red and blue,  
With shiny chrome spokes all shiny and new.  
On his handlebar perched a silver bell,  
A musical wonder that rang so well!  

Shane gave the bell a test-drive *PING!*  
Making the backyard robins sing!  
"Captain Shane is ready to ride!  
Let's pedal, Dad, by the riverside!"  

He placed his sneakers on pedals flat,  
Adjusted his cap and began to chat:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Biking Chant:**  
> *"Clickety-clack and pedal ahead,*  
> *Where rabbits run and robins tread!*  
> *Ring the bell with a ding-ding-dong,*  
> *Shane and Dad are twenty feet strong!"*  

---

### Scene 2: The Splashing Puddle of Puddle-Ville!

Off went the Bouncing Bicycle Brigade!  
Rolling beneath the maple shade!  
*Whirrrr-whirrrr-whirrrr* went the rubber wheels,  
Kicking up dust and gravel peels!  

Shane pedaled fast with his little knees,  
Racing the butterflies through the breeze.  
Dad Alex rolled along by his side,  
Guiding the turns on the neighborhood ride.  

Around the corner and down the street,  
Lay a giant puddle, wide and neat!  
Leftover rain from yesterday's storm,  
Shining like glass in the sunshine warm!  

"Steer around it?" Dad called out,  
With a teasing twinkle and playful shout.  
"No way, Dad!" Shane laughed with glee,  
"Puddle-ville is the place for me!"  

Shane pumped his pedals with all his might,  
A five-year-old rocket in speedy flight!  
He aimed his front wheel straight at the core,  
Ready to hear the water roar!  

*SPLOOOOOOOOOOSH!*  

A rooster-tail spray of water high,  
Splashed in the air toward the sunny sky!  
Droplets flew like crystal beads,  
Soaking the grass and the roadside weeds!  
Shane cheered as water splashed his shins,  
Showing the biggest of five-year-old grins!  

Dad Alex laughed and rang his bell,  
Singing the song that they loved so well:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Biking Chant:**  
> *"Clickety-clack and pedal ahead,*  
> *Where rabbits run and robins tread!*  
> *Ring the bell with a ding-ding-dong,*  
> *Shane and Dad are twenty feet strong!"*  

---

### Scene 3: The Crest of the Big Bumpy Hill!

Ahead rose the challenge, mighty and steep:  
The Big Bumpy Hill where the shadows sleep!  
A grassy green mountain that touched the sky,  
Where neighborhood kites would often fly.  

"Can we climb it?" Alex asked with a grin,  
"Or will the Big Bumpy Mountain win?"  
"We can do it!" Shane proudly said,  
Pedaling forward, head tilted ahead!  

*Push with the left! Push with the right!*  
Spinning those pedals with tiger might!  
Shane stood on his pedals, gritting his teeth,  
Leaving the valley far underneath!  
Alex cheered, "Keep going, Shane!  
You're powerful as a freight-train engine!"  

One final push... one final heave...  
A climb that nobody would believe!  
They rolled to the very top crest of the hill,  
Where the wind blew gentle and sweet and still.  

Below them the town was glowing bright,  
Bathed in the golden sunset light.  
Rooftops and tree-tops and winding trails,  
Painted in peaches and lilac veils!  

Shane parked his bicycle, stood up tall,  
Feeling as big as a palace wall!  
He raised his hand and struck the bell:  
*DING-DONG! DING-DONG!* it rang to tell!  

"We made it to the top!" Shane cried,  
Overflowing with happy pride!  
Alex wrapped Shane in a sunset hug,  
Two happy bikers, safe and snug.  

Together they shouted the victory song,  
As bedtime shadows stretched out long:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Biking Chant:**  
> *"Clickety-clack and home we go,*  
> *Down the hill in the sunset glow!*  
> *Rang the bell with a ding-ding-dong,*  
> *Shane and Dad conquered the mountain strong!"*
