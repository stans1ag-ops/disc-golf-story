# Rumpelstiltskin — Reference Notes (approved appearance anchors)

## The miller's daughter (`miller_daughter_ref.jpg`)
Beautiful young woman with long golden-brown hair in a thick braid, gentle hazel eyes, light freckles. Simple medieval peasant dress in soft blue with a cream apron, thin brown belt, brown boots. Kind, expressive face — worried or weeping in the straw chamber scenes.

## Rumpelstiltskin (`rumpelstiltskin_ref.jpg`)
A droll-looking little man, about three feet tall, odd proportions: large head, long pointed nose, sharp clever brown eyes, wild tufts of dark hair under a crooked patched pointed cap. Patched brown tunic and vest, baggy patched breeches, oversized buckled boots. Mischievous grin; never frightening.

## The King (`king_ref.jpg`)
Heavyset middle-aged greedy king: black beard streaked grey, golden crown with red gems, shrewd narrowed eyes. Rich deep-red robes trimmed with ermine fur, heavy gold chains with a medallion. Pompous, avaricious expression.

## The straw chamber (`straw_chamber_ref.jpg`)
Medieval stone palace chamber piled high with golden straw; wooden spinning wheel with reel at the center; small barred window with cold moonlight; heavy locked wooden door. Lonely nighttime mood.

## Style
Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper; fine pencil and ink contours; warm muted colors (warm ochre, cream, muted green, brown, soft blue) with cool moonlight in night scenes; expressive child-friendly faces. No text, logos, watermarks, collage panels, or photorealism.
