# Shane and the Magic Disc
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## The sunny trail

Shane carried his orange Roadrunner disc into Whispering Woods. Dad carried a pink one. Sunlight made bright spots on the path.

“First one to hear the chains?” Dad asked. Shane nodded and hurried to the tee. He liked the sound a disc made when it landed in the basket: ching!

## The first throw

Shane stood sideways and threw. His disc sailed over the grass and landed in the middle of the fairway. Dad gave him a high five. Dad’s pink disc landed near the basket.

Shane made a short toss. Ching! The chains rattled. “That’s my favorite sound,” he said. They picked up their discs and went deeper into the woods.

## Where did it go?

At the next tee, the path narrowed between tall trees. Shane threw hard. A gust of wind caught the Roadrunner and tipped it into the ferns.

Plip. Then silence.

Shane searched under one leaf, then another. No orange disc. Dad took his hand. “We’ll look together.” A faint ting came from farther in. They followed the sound past a mossy tree.

## A little help

In a clearing stood a basket wrapped in purple flowers. Beside it were a white squirrel, a small hedgehog, and a spotted baby deer. Shane’s Roadrunner lay between them.

“There it is!” Shane whispered. The squirrel lifted one edge. The hedgehog pushed from behind. The deer gave the disc a final nudge with its nose. Up it popped—and down into the basket. Ching!

## Thank you

Shane laughed. “You made it ring!” He picked up his disc and thanked each animal. The squirrel chirped. The deer dipped its head.

Dad tapped his pink disc against the orange one. The animals slipped back into the ferns, leaving the basket quiet again. Shane and Dad walked on. Every few steps, Shane looked over his shoulder, hoping to hear one more little ting.
