Shane and the Magic Disc

A sunny morning, two flying discs, and a forest full of wonder.

⏱️ 4 min read • 🎨 5 Illustrated Scenes • 🎯 Disc Golf Magic
Scene 1 • The Sunny Trail

The morning sun peeked through the tall pine trees, painting warm yellow stripes across the dirt trail. It was the perfect day for disc golf. Alex zipped up his bag and adjusted his cap, looking down at his five-year-old son, Shane. Shane was practically bouncing on his sneakers.

"Are you ready, buddy?" Alex asked with a big smile.

"Ready!" Shane cheered. He reached into his bag and pulled out his favorite treasure in the whole world. It was a disc as bright orange as a pumpkin, with a speedy bird printed right on top. "The Roadrunner is ready to fly, Dad!"

Alex smiled and pulled his own favorite disc from his bag. It was bright, bubblegum pink. "My pink flyer is ready too. Let's see who can hear the chains rattle first."

They walked side by side down the winding path of the Whispering Woods. The trees were giant giants, with rough brown bark and green branches that tickled the clouds. Every step went crunch, crunch, crunch on the fallen oak leaves.

Scene 2 • The First Throw

On the first hole, Alex showed Shane how to stand. "Stand sideways, buddy, just like a surfer on a wave. Pull it straight across your chest and snap your wrist."

Shane planted his sneakers into the grass. He held the orange Roadrunner tight, took a deep breath, and pointed his toe forward. "One, two, three... go!"

Whoosh!

Shane whipped his arm forward. The orange Roadrunner sailed through the air, spinning like a fiery wheel. It glided over the green clover, curved around a bush, and landed with a soft flop right in the middle of the fairway.

"Great throw, Shane!" Alex gave him a high five. "Look at that run! It really lives up to its name."

Alex took his turn, sending his pink disc high into the bright sky. It sailed smooth and clean, dropping down near the metal basket. When Shane made his final toss from up close, the orange disc slapped against the hanging chains with a loud, joyful CHA-CHING!

Shane did a silly victory dance, wiggling his hips. "Chains! I love that sound!"

Scene 3 • Into the Ferns

They played through the second and third holes, laughing and cheering as their discs zipped through the sunny woods. But hole four was different. The path led toward the deepest, quietest part of the forest, where the pine trees grew close together and the shadows were cool.

"This is a tricky one," Alex whispered, looking down the narrow green tunnel. "Keep it straight, Shane."

Shane stepped onto the tee pad. He wiped a speck of dust off his bright orange disc. He took a hop, swung his arm back, and let it rip.

The Roadrunner flew faster than ever before. It hummed through the air: zzzzzz! It zoomed past the first big tree. It zoomed past the second tree. But then, a playful gust of wind swooped down from the branches. The breeze grabbed the orange disc and tilted its wing.

"Uh oh!" Shane gasped.

The Roadrunner curved sharp to the right, flying straight into a thick patch of giant green ferns.

Plip.

Then, everything went quiet.

"Don't worry, buddy," Alex said, taking Shane's hand. "An orange disc can't hide in green ferns for long. Let's go track it down."

They stepped off the trail and walked into the ferns. The big leaves were so tall they tickled Shane's elbows. The sunlight broke through the canopy in glowing beams of gold.

Suddenly, Alex stopped. "Listen. Do you hear that?"

Shane listened with his very best listening ears.

It wasn't the wind. It was a tiny, sweet sound: ting-ting-ting! It sounded like hundreds of little fairy bells.

Hovering right in front of them was a trail of glowing, golden sparkles drifting above the moss. Holding hands, Alex and Shane followed the sparkles past a giant mossy oak tree.

Scene 4 • Woodland Magic

Shane gasped, and Alex’s eyes went wide.

Standing in a hidden, sunny circle was the metal disc golf basket. But bright purple flowers were wrapped around the pole, and the chains were glowing like silver starlight. Sitting right around the basket were three magical woodland friends: a little white squirrel with purple eyes, a round hedgehog wearing a crown of yellow dandelions, and a baby deer with spots that blinked like soft nightlights.

Right in the center of their circle was Shane’s orange Roadrunner.

The hedgehog was gently rolling the disc on its edge. The squirrel stood on its hind legs and let out a happy chirp. When the animals saw Shane and Alex, they didn't run away. The baby deer simply bowed its velvety head.

Alex knelt beside Shane and whispered, "Go ahead, buddy. Say hello."

Shane took a gentle step forward. "Hi," Shane whispered. "That's my Roadrunner."

The squirrel picked up the edge of the orange disc. The hedgehog gave it a soft push from behind. Together, the little critters rolled the Roadrunner across the soft moss toward a curved tree root. The baby deer gave it one last nudge with its wet nose.

Boing!

The orange Roadrunner bounced into the air, floated up, and dropped straight into the basket with a magical CLINK-CHING!

Shane jumped into the air. "A hole-in-one! An ace!"

Scene 5 • The Celebration

The forest animals cheered in tiny squeaks, clapping their paws as flower petals drifted down like colorful confetti. Shane ran forward and scooped up his disc. "Thank you!" he beamed.

The animals waved, hopped onto the baby deer’s back, and slipped into the ferns, leaving behind a swirl of warm, golden sparkles.

Alex scooped Shane up into a giant bear hug. "Did you see that, Dad?!" Shane laughed, holding his disc high. "The forest animals helped Roadrunner score an ace!"

Alex pulled out his pink disc and tapped it against Shane's orange one with a click. "That was the most magical throw in disc golf history."

With the Roadrunner tucked safely under Shane's arm, they walked toward hole five together, ready for whatever magic the woods had in store next.
