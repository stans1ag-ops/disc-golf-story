# Hansel and Gretel — Reference Notes (approved appearance anchors)

## Hansel and Gretel (`hansel_gretel_ref.jpg`)
Hansel: brave boy about eight, tousled brown hair, patched brown jacket, patched trousers, scuffed boots. Gretel: sweet girl about six, long golden braids tied with red ribbons, bright blue eyes, simple soft-green peasant dress with cream apron. They hold hands. Kind, expressive faces.

## The Witch (`witch_ref.jpg`)
A very, very old woman: wrinkled face, hooked nose, red eyes, straggly grey hair under a dark tattered shawl, leaning on a wooden crutch, tattered dark-purple dress. Falsely sweet expression with a sly cunning glint. Spooky but child-safe; never gory.

## The Gingerbread House (`gingerbread_house_ref.jpg`)
A little cottage deep in the dark pine forest: walls of bread, roof covered in cakes, pastries, macarons and meringues, windows of clear sparkling sugar, candy-cane door posts, gumdrop path, warm inviting glow from the windows. Enchanting and tempting.

## The Deep Forest (`forest_ref.jpg`)
Towering ancient pines and oaks, thick undergrowth, a narrow winding path disappearing into shadowy depths, shafts of pale light through the canopy. Mysterious, a little foreboding.

## Style
Traditional watercolor storybook illustration on lightly textured cream paper; fine pencil and ink contours; warm muted colors (warm ochre, cream, muted green, brown, soft blue) with deep green forest shadows; expressive child-friendly faces. No text, logos, watermarks, collage panels, or photorealism.
