# The Bedtime Toot-Tastrophe
*A Rhyming Bedtime Story*

The moon was a crescent, the stars were in sight,
And Mt. Pleasant was settling into the night.
Young Shane brushed his teeth till they sparkled like snow,
Then hopped in his bed with a five-year-old glow.
His blankets were pulled to his chin, warm and neat,
Tucked snug from his ears right on down to his feet.
In walked his mom, Theresa, smiling and kind,
With glasses in place and a story in mind.
She sat on the mattress and opened the book,
And gave little Shane her sweet motherly look.
She opened her mouth for the very first word,
When suddenly—TOOT!—came the weirdest sound heard.
A squeak like a mouse on a tiny brass horn!
The funniest noise since the day Shane was born.
Shane opened his eyes and he poked out his head:
"Did someone just blow a kazoo near my bed?"
Theresa turned pink. "Oh, dear me, that was odd!
A squeak in the floorboards!" she claimed with a nod.
She cleared out her throat and she straightened her spine,
"Now back to our tale of the brave porcupine..."
She leaned just an inch to turn over page three,
When out slipped a rumble as loud as the sea!
PBBBBBT-whistle-POP! went the seat of the chair,
A gust that tossed ribbons of dust in the air.
Shane burst out laughing. "Mom, that wasn't the floor!
That sounded like thunder behind the front door!"
Theresa pressed tight on her lips with a blush,
"Hush, sweetheart," she whispered, "now listen and hush!"
She pinched her knees shut and she held in her breath;
She tried to stay quieter than silent death.
She squeezed and she squirmed and she counted to eight,
Her tummy was rumbling like heavy freight.
"I'm fine," Theresa said, "it's just broccoli from lunch,
Or maybe the fiber bar snacks that I munch!"
She stood up to pat Shane's soft pillow in place,
Determined to salvage a straight, solemn face.
She bent at the waist, gave the pillow a fluff—
And HONK! went her belly! A giant steam puff!
TOOT-A-LOO! BUBBLE-POP! PARP-ITY-WHEEZE!
The window blinds rattled from Mom's little breeze!
Now Shane lost his marbles; he rolled on the sheets,
His giggles as sweet as the baker's best treats.
He kicked up his heels and he clutched at his vest:
"Mom, you're an orchestra! You are the best!"
Theresa looked down, and she started to grin;
There wasn't a chance she could hold it all in.
She chuckled, she snorted, and out came three more:
Pip-pop! and a bop! as she stepped toward the door!
"Well, Shane," chuckled Mom, wiping tears from her eyes,
"I didn't intend for bedtime surprise!
It seems that my motor just won't settle down,
I might just toot-toot all the way across town!"
Shane sat right up and he gave her a hug,
Wrapped safe in his blankets so cozy and snug.
"Don't worry, sweet Mom, it's the best book by far—
You toot like a rocket that shoots for a star!"
Theresa smoothed back Shane's neat spiky brown hair,
And gave him a kiss on his cheek right then there.
"Goodnight, little buddy. Sweet dreams until dawn."
She gave a big stretch and a sleepy, slow yawn.
She switched off the lamp with a gentle soft click,
And tiptoed outside on her toes very quick.
And just as the door closed, as quiet could be...
One final soft toot floated out full of glee.
