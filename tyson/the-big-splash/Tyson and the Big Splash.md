# Tyson and the Big Splash
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## Morning at the lake

Tyson and his dad, Alex, went to the lake. Tyson had his rod. Dad had the worms. Both had their life jackets on.

“I will catch a big fish!” said Tyson. Dad put a worm on the hook. Tyson cast his line. Plop! His red float sat on the water.

They sat. They waited. A duck swam past. “Do you know where the fish are?” Tyson asked it. “Quack,” said the duck. “That was not much help,” said Dad.

## The stick fish

Then the float went down. “Dad! I have one!” Tyson held his rod up. He turned the reel. The line pulled hard. “It must be huge!” he said.

Dad got the net. Tyson reeled some more. Up came a stick.

## One stick and one hat

Tyson stared at it. Dad stared at it. “That fish has a lot of legs,” said Dad.

Tyson laughed so hard his hat fell off. Dad caught it in the net. “One stick and one hat!” said Tyson.

## Small fish, big splash

He tried again. This time, he cast near some weeds. Plop. Wait. Tug! A small fish flashed in the sun. Dad helped bring it in.

“My first real fish!” said Tyson. It was no bigger than his hand. Dad took a photo. Then they let the fish go. Splash! Water hit Dad right on the nose.

Tyson grinned. “Small fish. Big splash!” On the way home, Dad asked, “What was your best catch?” “The fish,” said Tyson. “But you caught a pretty nice hat.”

> **Say it with Tyson:** Plop!
