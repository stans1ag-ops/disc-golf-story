# Tyson and Mario’s Missing Star
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## The green pipe

Tyson and his mom, Theresa, found a green pipe. A red cap popped out. “Mario!” said Tyson. Mario climbed up. “I need help. My star is gone!” “We can look,” said Mom.

## Whoosh!

They went down the pipe. Whoosh! They landed on soft grass. There were big blocks in the sky. “Where did you see it last?” asked Tyson. “By that hill,” said Mario.

They went up the hill. Something yellow shone by a rock. “My star!” said Mario. But it was a banana peel. “Good thing we did not step on it,” said Mom. They put it in a bin.

## Yoshi’s thorn

Then Tyson heard a sound. Sniff. Sniff. Yoshi sat by a bush. He had a tear on his cheek. “What is wrong?” asked Tyson. Yoshi pointed to his foot. A small thorn was stuck in it.

Mom took out the thorn with care. Tyson held Yoshi’s hand. “All done,” said Mom.

## The star

Yoshi stood up. He gave a happy hop. Ding! A bright star fell from the bush. “There it is!” said Mario. Yoshi had been sitting right next to it. Tyson picked up the star. It felt warm in his hands. He gave it to Mario.

“You found my star!” said Mario. “And we found someone who needed help,” said Mom. Mario led them back to the pipe. Yoshi waved. Whoosh! They were home. Tyson looked at his shoes. One had a big green spot. “Grass from Mario’s world!” he said. Mom smiled. “Let’s keep that part off the couch.”

> **Say it with Tyson:** Whoosh!
