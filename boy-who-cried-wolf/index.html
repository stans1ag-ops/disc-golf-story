The Boy Who Cried Wolf

An Aesop’s fable • A bedtime tale about a bored shepherd boy, two false alarms, and why nobody believes a liar.

Scene 1 • The Bored Shepherd Boy

There once was a shepherd boy who was bored as he sat on the hillside watching the village sheep.

Scene 2 • A Lonely Day

It was rather lonely for him all day, so he played on his shepherd's pipe, and thought upon a plan by which he could get a little company and some excitement.

Scene 3 • The Mischievous Idea

To amuse himself he took a great breath and sang out...

🧒 The Boy
“Wolf! The wolf is chasing the sheep!”
Scene 4 • The Villagers Rush Up

The villagers came running up the hill to help the boy drive the wolf away. But when they arrived at the top of the hill, they found no wolf.

Scene 5 • The Boy Laughs

The boy laughed at the sight of their angry faces.

🧑‍🌾 The Villagers
“Don't cry 'wolf', shepherd boy, when there's no wolf!” — They went grumbling back down the hill.
Scene 6 • A Stern Warning

The boy felt a little ashamed, but not for long. For the joke had been such fun, he could think of nothing else all day.

Scene 7 • He Cries Wolf Again

Later, the boy sang out again, "Wolf! The wolf is chasing the sheep!" To his naughty delight, he watched the villagers run up the hill to help him drive the wolf away.

Scene 8 • Grumbling Down Again

When the villagers saw no wolf they sternly said, "Save your frightened song for when there is really something wrong! Don't cry 'wolf' when there is NO wolf!" But the boy just grinned and watched them go grumbling down the hill once more.

Scene 9 • The Last Warning

That evening the boy promised himself he would never play the trick again. But a promise made to yourself is easy to forget.

Scene 10 • A Real Wolf

Later, he saw a REAL wolf prowling about his flock, come out from the dark forest.

Scene 11 • Wolf! Wolf!

Alarmed, he leaped to his feet and sang out as loudly as he could, "Wolf! Wolf!"

Scene 12 • Nobody Comes

But the villagers thought he was trying to fool them again, and so they didn't come. "He cannot fool us again," they said, and went on with their work.

Scene 13 • Sunset Worry

At sunset, everyone wondered why the shepherd boy hadn't returned to the village with their sheep. They went up the hill to find the boy.

Scene 14 • Found Weeping

They found him weeping.

😢 The Boy
“There really was a wolf here! The flock has scattered! I cried out, 'Wolf!' Why didn't you come?”
Scene 15 • Nobody Believes a Liar

An old man tried to comfort the boy as they walked back to the village. "We'll help you look for the lost sheep in the morning," he said, putting his arm around the youth. And that is the end of the tale.

🧓 The Wise Old Man
“Nobody believes a liar, even when he is telling the truth!”
💭 Bedtime Chat

What should the boy have done instead of crying “wolf” for fun?

🗣️ Tell the Truth Lying for fun cost the boy his flock. Telling the truth, even when it’s boring, keeps people’s trust — and trust is hard to win back.
🎺 Find a Better Game The boy was bored, and boredom made him mischievous. When you’re bored, find a real game to play — like the boy’s pipe — instead of tricking people.
