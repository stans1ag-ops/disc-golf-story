# The Houghton Lake Pontoon Patrol
*A Bedtime Adventure for Shane and Alex*
*Reading Time: 4-5 minutes • Rhyming & Alliterative Bedtime Story*

---

### Scene 1: The Peppy Pontoon Sets Sail

The summer sun shone sizzling bright,  
The sparkling lake was warm and light.  
Up in Michigan, calm and wide,  
Lay Houghton Lake in summer pride!  
Along the dock with bouncing stride,  
Walked little Shane by Alex's side.  

Shane buckled snug his jacket tight,  
Bright orange vest of safety light!  
He gave his dad a high-five smack,  
With yellow tackle on his back.  
"Today’s the day!" cheered five-year Shane,  
"We'll catch a fish as big as a crane!"  

Dad Alex chuckled, turned the key,  
The motor hummed with bubbly glee.  
*Chugga-chugga-chug, purr-purr-pop!*  
The peppy pontoon left the dock!  
It skimmed the shimmering, shallow blue,  
With captain Shane and his favorite crew.  

Alex pointed toward the pine-tree shore,  
Where giant bass might splash and roar.  
"Get ready, buddy, grab your stick,  
And do our fishing magic trick!"  

Shane clapped his hands, he couldn't wait,  
He shook his hips and named the bait:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Fishing Chant:**  
> *"Wiggle waggle, tickle and tap!*  
> *Fishy, fishy, take a nap!*  
> *Give the line a splishy-swoosh,*  
> *Reel him in with a mighty PULL-OOSH!"*  

---

### Scene 2: The Bobber's Bouncing Dance

They cut the engine near the reeds,  
Among the floating waterweeds.  
The water rested, soft and still,  
Beneath the sunny breezy hill.  
Shane planted sneakers firm and flat  
Beside his dad’s wide fishing hat.  

"Now hold it steady, aim it true,"  
Said Alex, smiling down at two  
Bright eager eyes that watched the line  
Sail through the air so sleek and fine.  

*Zzzzz-CLICK!* The cast was neat and round,  
The red-white bobber hit the sound  
With a tiny splash—*plink-plop, plink-plee!*  
Right in the middle of the sea!  

Shane whispered low, as quiet as mice,  
"Come on, big fish, be sweet and nice..."  
The bobber danced upon the lake,  
A gentle little wiggle-wake.  

Then suddenly—*BLOOP!*—it went below!  
"Look Dad! Look Dad! It's starting to go!"  
The tip of the rod bent like a bow!  
Shane stamped his feet with merry zeal,  
And grabbed the golden shiny reel.  

Together they shouted the magic spell,  
As loud as a chiming church-yard bell:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Fishing Chant:**  
> *"Wiggle waggle, tickle and tap!*  
> *Fishy, fishy, take a nap!*  
> *Give the line a splishy-swoosh,*  
> *Reel him in with a mighty PULL-OOSH!"*  

---

### Scene 3: The Great Green Splash!

*Crackle, click, and crank-crank-crank!*  
Stronger than an army tank!  
Alex wrapped his hands around  
Shane's little grip upon the ground.  
"Hold on, buddy! Feel the fight!  
Reel that fish with all your might!"  

The water boiled! The bubbles burst!  
Who'd reach the sunny surface first?  
With one huge heave and a laughing cry,  
A monster leaped into the sky!  

*SPLOOOOOSH!*  

A giant, goofy, grand green bass,  
With scales as shiny as silver glass!  
He wore a lily pad like a crown,  
And smiled at Shane without a frown.  
He wiggled his fins, he flapped his tail,  
And gave a splash like a happy whale!  

"You got him, Shane!" cheered proud dad Alex,  
"A record catch from east to west!"  
Shane laughed so hard his tummy shook,  
As they popped the flopper off the hook.  

They blew the fish a friendly kiss,  
And let him dive in watery bliss.  
*Plop-splish-swish!* Away he swam,  
The happiest fish in Michigan!  

Shane sang the chant one final time,  
As afternoon began to chime:  

> **Shane and Dad's Silly Fishing Chant:**  
> *"Wiggle waggle, tickle and tap!*  
> *Fishy, fishy, took his nap!*  
> *Pontoon patrol has won the day,*  
> *Houghton Lake, hip-hip-hooray!"*  

Alex scooped Shane into a captain's hug,  
Safe and cozy, warm and snug.  
And the pontoon purred as the sun sank low,  
Homeward bound in the golden glow.
