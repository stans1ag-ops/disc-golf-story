# Shane and the Big Hill
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## Click, ring, roll

Click went Shane’s helmet buckle. Dad checked the strap, then the brakes on Shane’s red and blue bike. “Ready?” he asked.

Shane rang his little silver bell. Ding! “Now I am.” They rolled down the path together. Dad rode close enough to talk, but Shane chose the way.

At the bend, Shane saw a puddle across the trail. He slowed down. “Can I go through it?” Dad checked that it was shallow. “You can. Keep your wheels straight.”

> **Say it with Shane:** Click. Ring. Roll.

## The puddle

Shane aimed for the middle. The front wheel touched the water. Then the back wheel followed.

Splash! Cold drops flew onto his legs. Dad got a few drops too. Shane laughed and rang his bell. “Did you see that?” “Every bit of it,” said Dad.

Around the next turn stood the Big Hill. It looked much taller from the bottom. Shane stopped smiling for a moment. “That’s a lot of uphill,” he said. Dad stopped beside him. “We can go slowly. I’ll stay with you.”

> **Say it with Shane:** One more push.

## The top

Shane pedaled. The hill pushed back. He pedaled again. His legs felt tired, and the top still looked far away.

“One more push,” said Dad. Shane pushed. Then one more. Then one more. At last his front wheel rolled onto flat ground.

From the top, he could see the puddle shining below. It looked tiny now. “I did the whole hill,” he said, almost to himself.

Dad smiled. Shane rang his bell three times. Ding! Ding! Ding! They rested while the evening sky turned orange. Then they took the easy path home.

> **Say it with Shane:** I did it!
