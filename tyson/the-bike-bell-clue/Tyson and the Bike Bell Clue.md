# Tyson and the Bike Bell Clue
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## Ding, ding!

Tyson and his dad, Alex, put on their helmets. They rode their bikes on the park path. Ding, ding! Tyson rang his bell.

Up ahead, a boy stood by a bench. He was looking under it. “Did you lose something?” asked Tyson.

## The search

“My toy car,” said the boy. “It is red. It was in my pocket.” Tyson and Dad stopped their bikes. They got off to help. “Where did you go?” asked Tyson. “To the swings. Then here.”

They looked by the swings. No car. They looked by the slide. No car. Dad found a red leaf. “Does your car have a stem?” he asked. The boy gave a small smile. “No.”

## Cars roll

Tyson looked down the path. There was a hill between the swings and the bench. “Cars roll,” he said. “Maybe it went down there.” They walked to the foot of the hill.

Tyson saw a red dot in the grass. He bent down. A bottle cap. “Not yet,” he said. Then he saw four tiny wheels. They stuck up from behind a pine cone. “Found it!”

## The car finder

The boy hugged his car. “Thanks!” He rolled it on the bench. It went fast. “Your car had a big trip,” said Tyson. Tyson and Dad got back on their bikes. “You used a good clue,” said Dad.

Tyson rang his bell. Ding, ding! “What is that for?” asked Dad. “It means the car finder is back at work!” Just then, Dad dropped his glove. Tyson laughed. “Another job!”

> **Say it with Tyson:** Ding, ding!
