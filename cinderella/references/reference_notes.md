# Cinderella — reference notes

Style: soft watercolor children's book illustration, warm muted palette, traditional watercolor on lightly textured cream paper, fine gentle outlines, landscape composition near 16:9 for scenes. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no photorealism.

- Cinderella (hearth look): kind lovely young woman, ash-blonde hair tied back, old grey kirtle dress, wooden clogs, small ash smudge on cheek, gentle hopeful expression. Ball look: same face and hair worn up, in a splendid dress of gold and silver (night 1), a still more splendid dress (night 2), and on night 3 a dress of unmatched splendour with slippers all of gold. Slippers: nights 1-2 embroidered silk and silver; night 3 pure gold.
- Stepmother: stern elegant older woman, dark plum gown, haughty. Stepsisters: two beautiful vain young women in rich colorful dresses, proud expressions, chins high.
- Prince: handsome kind young prince, dark hair, deep blue and gold royal coat with red sash.
- Hazel tree at the mother's grave: young green hazel tree over a simple grave mound in a churchyard garden; the white bird (small white dove-like bird) perched on a branch. The two white pigeons from the lentil scenes are the same kind of bird.
- Settings: old-fashioned kitchen with big hearth and cinders; the king's castle ballroom; garden with pigeon-house and a large pear tree; the castle steps (pitch on the third night).
- Ending policy (pending Alex): original Grimm ending — stepsisters cut toe/heel (implied in scenes, never shown), doves sing "blood on her shoe" (no blood shown in images), wedding-day dove justice (told in text only; final image is the wedding celebration with the doves on Cinderella's shoulders).
