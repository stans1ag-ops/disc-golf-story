# The Red Sled Ride
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## Up the hill

Snow covered the path, the trees, and the little bench at the bottom of the hill. Shane wore his red coat and pom-pom hat. Dad pulled their red sled by its rope.

“This hill looks bigger in snow,” Shane said. They climbed together. Halfway up, Shane stopped to catch his breath. Dad stopped too. “We have plenty of time,” he said.

At the top, Shane brushed snow from the sled. Dad sat behind him. Shane tucked his mittens around the sides. “Ready?” Dad asked. Shane gave a serious nod.

> **Say it with Shane:** Ready, set... whoosh!

## Three bumps

Dad gave the sled a push. Down they went.

The first bump made Shane bounce. “One!” The second made Dad’s hat slip over one eye. “Two!” On the third bump, the sled lifted for a tiny moment, then landed in soft snow. “Three!” they shouted together.

The sled slid into a drift and stopped. Snow flew over their boots. Shane lay back and looked at the white sky. Then he laughed so hard Dad started laughing too.

“Again?” Shane asked. Dad looked up at the long hill. “After we warm up.”

> **Say it with Shane:** One, two, three!

## Warm hands

They made snow angels beside the sled. Shane’s angel had small wings. Dad’s was so big that one wing touched the path.

On the bench, Dad opened a thermos of cocoa. Shane held his cup with both mittens. A marshmallow floated on top. After one sip, a white smudge sat above his lip.

Dad pointed to his own mouth. Shane wiped the wrong side of his face. Dad laughed and helped him find it.

The hill was quiet now. They watched the last sledders head home. “We can ride again tomorrow,” Dad said. Shane leaned against him and took one more warm sip.

> **Say it with Shane:** Tomorrow.
