# Tyson and the Fish That Said Plop
*A bedtime adventure for Tyson, Mom, and Dad*
*About 3–4 minutes to read aloud*

## Squirtle says hello

Tyson and his mom, Theresa, went to the pond. They wore life jackets and sat on the dock. Tyson cast his line. Plop!

A round head popped up. “Squirtle!” said Tyson. The little Pokémon waved. “Do you like to fish?” asked Mom. Squirtle nodded. Then it ducked under the water.

## The leaf prank

Tyson’s float bobbed. Once. Twice. He lifted his rod. At the end of the line was a wet leaf. Squirtle popped up and clapped. “That is not a fish!” said Tyson.

Squirtle looked at the leaf. Then it looked at Tyson. It gave a big grin. Tyson cast again. Plop! The float bobbed. Tyson reeled in. This time, he had a small twig. Squirtle clapped even harder. “Are you putting things on my hook?” asked Tyson. Squirtle hid its face. But Tyson could see its grin.

## The leaf-boat game

Mom laughed. “I think we have a pond prank.” Tyson set his rod down. “I have a new game,” he said. Mom put a leaf on the water. It was shaped like a boat. “Can you push it to that reed?”

Squirtle blew a tiny puff of water. The leaf spun in a circle. “Too much!” said Tyson. Squirtle tried a soft puff. The leaf slid to the reed. “You did it!”

## One happy splash

Then Squirtle gave one happy splash. Plop! A drop landed on Tyson’s nose. Mom held out a towel. “Did we catch any fish?” she asked. “No,” said Tyson. “But we found a very silly friend.”

> **Say it with Tyson:** Plop!
