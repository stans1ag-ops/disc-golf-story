# 📖 Shane & Alex's Bedtime Stories

A collection of illustrated bedtime stories starring **Alex** (Dad) and **Shane** (5 years old). Each story is crafted specifically for cozy 4-5 minute bedtime reading, featuring:
- 🎨 **Hand-Drawn Storybook Illustrations**: Gentle watercolor & ink art style capturing real memories and character likenesses.
- 🔤 **Playful Alliteration**: Joyous tongue-twisting fun that rolls off the tongue.
- 🎶 **Rhyming Verse & Rhythmic Flow**: Room-on-the-Broom & Dr. Seuss style bedtime cadence.
- 🗣️ **Silly Repeated Phrases**: Catchy chants that Shane can shout out loud together with Dad!
- 📱 **Mobile & Bedtime Ready**: Responsive design, Lightbox image zoom, Text-to-Speech (Read Aloud), and Day / Sunset Sepia / Bedtime Night themes.

---

## 🌟 The 5 Featured Adventures

### 1. 🚤 The Houghton Lake Pontoon Patrol
- **Theme**: Fishing on the pontoon at Houghton Lake, Michigan
- **Silly Chant**:
  > *"Wiggle waggle, tickle and tap! Fishy, fishy, take a nap! Give the line a splishy-swoosh, reel him in with a mighty PULL-OOSH!"*
- **Illustrations**:
  - Cruising on the pontoon with Captain Shane in his orange life vest
  - Watching the bobber go *bloop* in the water
  - The big friendly green bass leaping out with a splash

### 2. 🥏 The Daring Disc Golf Dash
- **Theme**: Playing disc golf in Whispering Woods
- **Silly Chant**:
  > *"Zippity-zap and flippity-flop, this zooming disc will never stop! Through the tall trees and around the bend, chains go CHING at the journey's end!"*
- **Illustrations**:
  - The surfer stance on the sunny tee pad
  - The orange Roadrunner disc soaring on a loop-de-loop past a friendly squirrel
  - The *CLINK-CHING* hole-in-one ace celebration with woodland friends

### 3. 🚲 The Bouncing Bicycle Brigade
- **Theme**: Biking adventures and conquering the Big Bumpy Hill
- **Silly Chant**:
  > *"Clickety-clack and pedal ahead, where rabbits run and robins tread! Ring the bell with a ding-ding-dong, Shane and Dad are twenty feet strong!"*
- **Illustrations**:
  - Helmet buckle *CLICK!* and driveway bike inspection
  - Rocketing through the giant park puddle with a *SPLOOOSH!*
  - Conquering the summit of the hill at sunset and ringing the bell

### 4. 🛷 The Super-Duper Snowy Sledding Slide
- **Theme**: Sledding in the snow down Mount Snowball
- **Silly Chant**:
  > *"Swoosh-a-loo and whoosh-a-lee, down the white hill go we! Hold on tight and steer it straight, flying like a rocket past the garden gate!"*
- **Illustrations**:
  - Bundled-up little astronauts pulling the red sled up the sparkling hill
  - The high-speed *SWOOSH!* with white powder flying
  - Hot cocoa with a giant marshmallow foam mustache and snow angels

### 5. 🃏 The Wild Uno Uproar
- **Theme**: Cozy bedtime card battle by the fire in dinosaur pajamas
- **Silly Chant**:
  > *"Shuffle and slap and lay it neat, Shane’s got cards that can’t be beat! Red, yellow, green, or blue, Draw-Four, Dad, and UNO too!"*
- **Illustrations**:
  - Shane peeking over a fan of cards with his cheeky poker face
  - The theatrical *SLAP!* of the rainbow Wild Draw-Four as Dad gasps in defeat
