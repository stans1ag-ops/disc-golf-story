[SCENE 01: Chapter 1: The Great Siege of Troy]
[NARRATOR] The Great Island Road Trip: The Odyssey. Chapter One: The Giant Wooden Horse and the Forgotten Map.
[NARRATOR] Long ago, on the sparkling blue waters of the Mediterranean Sea, lived the cleverest captain in the world. His name was [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/), and he ruled a sunny, rocky island called [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/). [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) wasn't the biggest or the strongest king, but he had a brain that worked like a Swiss watch. Whenever there was a problem that nobody else could solve, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) would tap his chin, squint one eye, and say:
[ODYSSEUS] Hold on. I have a ridiculous idea.
[NARRATOR] For ten long years, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) and his crew of goofy, loyal sailors had been away from home competing in the Great Siege of Troy. It wasn't a battle of sharp things; it was the ultimate championship of prank wars and castle-capturing. The walls of Troy were fifty feet high and made of solid granite. For ten years, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) and his friends tried catapulting water balloons, building giant springboards, and digging tunnels that accidentally ended up in the royal bakery. Nothing worked.

[SCENE 02: The Giant Smiling Wooden Horse]
[NARRATOR] Finally, on a Tuesday afternoon, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) tapped his chin.
[ODYSSEUS] We are going to build a horse!
[NARRATOR] he announced to his first mate, a big, lovable guy named [Eurylochus](/jʊɹˈɪləkəs/) who was always thinking about lunch.
[EURYLOCHUS] A horse, Captain? Like a pony?
[NARRATOR] [Eurylochus](/jʊɹˈɪləkəs/) asked, scratching his beard.
[ODYSSEUS] No. A wooden horse the size of a three-story barn. We'll put it on giant wooden squeaky wheels, paint a big bow on the front that says CONGRATULATIONS, YOU WIN!, and hide inside its belly with a hundred party horns and silly-string blasters!
[NARRATOR] And that is exactly what they did. They hammered together old ship planks, climbed inside through a secret trapdoor in the horse's tummy, and waited. When the Trojans looked over their giant stone wall and saw the beaches empty except for a massive, smiling wooden horse, they cheered! They rolled the heavy horse right through their front gates and threw a massive dance party.

[SCENE 03: The Golden Trophy and Setting Sail]
[NARRATOR] At midnight, when the Trojans were all worn out from dancing and fast asleep on the cobblestones, CLICK! went the trapdoor. [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) and his crew dropped down on ropes, blew their brass horns, and covered the entire town square in brightly colored banners. The King of Troy woke up, laughed until his sides hurt, and handed [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) the Golden Trophy of Troy.
[NARRATOR] Standing on the bow of his flagship, a sleek wooden galley painted bright red with big white sails, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) cheered:
[ODYSSEUS] Alright, boys! Set a course for [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/)! My wife [Penelope](/pənˈɛləpi/) is probably making her famous honey-nut pancakes, and my son [Telemachus](/təlˈɛməkəs/) is probably tall enough to ride a donkey by now. With a good wind, we'll be home by Friday!
[NARRATOR] Everyone cheered. [Eurylochus](/jʊɹˈɪləkəs/) unpacked a celebratory wheel of cheddar cheese. Nobody knew that Friday was going to take ten whole years to arrive.

[SCENE 04: Chapter 2: The Island of Pink Palm Trees]
[NARRATOR] Chapter Two: The Island of the Super-Sleepy Berry Eaters.
[NARRATOR] For the first three days, the wind was polite and pushed their red ship smoothly across the waves. On the fourth day, however, the sky turned the color of grape jelly, and a howling gale spun their ship around like a plastic tub toy. For forty-eight hours, nobody could see a thing except flying sea spray.
[NARRATOR] When the clouds finally parted, the compass had bounced overboard, and the ship was floating beside a strange, tropical island covered in glowing pink palm trees. The air smelled like warm cinnamon rolls and vanilla frosting.
[NARRATOR] [Eurylochus](/jʊɹˈɪləkəs/)'s stomach rumbled so loudly it echoed off the cliffs.
[EURYLOCHUS] Ooh, Captain, we're out of fresh water, and my cheese wheel fell in the bilge. Let's go see who's baking!
[NARRATOR] [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) squinted through his brass spyglass.
[ODYSSEUS] Stay on the ship. I'll send three scouts—[Polites](/pəlˈItiːz/), [Elpenor](/ɛlpˈiːnɔːɹ/), and Little Tim—to ask for directions and fill the water barrels. Be back in twenty minutes!
[NARRATOR] The three scouts hopped into a rowboat, paddled to the sugary white beach, and wandered into the pink jungle. Twenty minutes passed. Then forty minutes. Then two hours.
[EURYLOCHUS] They probably found a buffet.
[NARRATOR] [Eurylochus](/jʊɹˈɪləkəs/) grumbled.

[SCENE 05: The Super-Sleepy Berry Eaters]

Lounging on the tubes were the island locals, wearing flower crowns and eating glowing, neon-blue berries the size of plums.
[NARRATOR] Right in the middle of the pond were [Polites](/pəlˈItiːz/), [Elpenor](/ɛlpˈiːnɔːɹ/), and Little Tim. They had kicked off their boots, put on coconut-shell sunglasses, and were floating around in circles with huge, goofy grins on their faces.
[ODYSSEUS] Boys! We have to go home to [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/)! Remember? Our families? The pancakes?

[SAILOR] [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/)? Who needs [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/), Cap? Look at this cloud. It looks like a fluffy marshmallow sheep. Have a berry, Cap. It tastes like a nap.

[SAILOR] No! Let's never row again! Rowing makes my arms tired. Let's just float here until we turn into prunes!
[NARRATOR] [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) realized the glowing Lotus Berries were magical procrastination fruit. One bite, and you forgot your homework, your chores, and your own address—all you wanted to do was lounge around and watch clouds forever.
[NARRATOR] Holding his breath so he wouldn't inhale the sweet berry juice, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) pulled out his rope. Like a master cowboy, he twirled a lasso and snagged all three inner tubes at once.
[SAILOR] Hey! You're ruining the vibe!
[NARRATOR] [Elpenor](/ɛlpˈiːnɔːɹ/) whined as [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) dragged the three giggling, yawning sailors down the beach like a train of pool floats. He tossed them into the rowboat, tied them gently to the rowing benches so they wouldn't jump back into the water to chase butterflies, and shouted to the rest of the crew:
[ODYSSEUS] Row! Row before we all decide to take a three-year nap!
[NARRATOR] Within ten minutes, the Lotus Berry magic wore off, and the three scouts blinked, looked at their flower crowns, and asked why they were tied to the benches.

[SCENE 14: Chapter 6: The Grandpa Disguise and Faithful Argos]
[NARRATOR] Chapter Six: The Ultimate Trick Shot and the Pancake Reunion.
[NARRATOR] After surviving a stormy shipwreck where [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) had to paddle the last fifty miles on an inflatable raft made of lashed-together drift logs, he finally washed ashore on the sandy beaches of [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/)! Twenty years had passed since he first set sail.
[NARRATOR] Before running straight up to his castle, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) met his old friend [Athena](/əθˈiːnə/), the goddess of wisdom, who was disguised as a shepherd.
[ATHENA] Hold your horses, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/). While you were gone, a hundred greedy, lazy guys called the Suitors moved into your living room! They think you're lost at sea, so they're eating all your groceries, leaving their dirty socks on the sofa, and competing to see who gets to become the new King of [Ithaca](/ˈɪθəkə/)! Today, your wife [Penelope](/pənˈɛləpi/) is holding an archery contest to finally get rid of them. Whoever can string your old bow and shoot a suction-cup arrow cleanly through the round rings of twelve axe handles lined up in a row wins!
[ODYSSEUS] Nobody can bend my old bow except me! It has a two-hundred-pound draw weight! Wait... if I walk in wearing my royal cape, those hundred freeloaders will lock the doors!
[NARRATOR] [Athena](/əθˈiːnə/) smiled and tapped [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) on the head with an olive branch. SHIMMER! Instantly, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) was disguised as a shaky, wrinkled old grandpa wearing a patched tweed bathrobe and fuzzy slippers.
[NARRATOR] When [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) shuffled into his own palace dining hall, he found a heartwarming surprise waiting by the front door: his faithful old hound dog, [Argos](/ˈɑːɹɡOs/), who was now twenty years old with white fur around his muzzle. Even through the magical grandpa disguise, [Argos](/ˈɑːɹɡOs/) sniffed once, recognized his best friend's scent, and wagged his tail so hard his whole back end wiggled before curling up on [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/)'s foot.

[SCENE 15: The Ultimate Twelve-Ring Trick Shot]
[NARRATOR] Inside the hall, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/)'s son [Telemachus](/təlˈɛməkəs/)—now a tall, brave young man—was trying to get the rowdy Suitors to use coasters. One by one, the arrogant, muscle-bound Suitors stepped up to try and bend [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/)'s legendary wooden bow.
[NARRATOR] The loudest Suitor, a pompous guy named [Antinous](/æntˈɪnOəs/) who was wearing three gold chains and eating [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/)'s favorite grapes, pulled on the bowstring until his face turned the color of a ripe tomato. BOING! The bow didn't budge an inch, and [Antinous](/æntˈɪnOəs/) fell flat on his bottom into a bowl of mashed potatoes.
[ANTINOUS] This bow is defective! Nobody on Earth can string this thing!
[NARRATOR] [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) shuffled forward in his fuzzy slippers, speaking in a creaky grandpa voice.
[ODYSSEUS] Excuse me, young fellas. Mind if an old-timer gives it a whirl? Back in my day, I used to have a pretty good arm.
[NARRATOR] The Suitors laughed until they cried.
[ANTINOUS] Sure, Grandpa! Try not to pull a hamstring!
[NARRATOR] [Telemachus](/təlˈɛməkəs/), who had already figured out the wink his dad gave him earlier, handed the heavy bow to the old man. With effortless grace, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) hooked one end of the bow against his boot, bent the massive wood like a piece of licorice, and slipped the string into the notch. He plucked the string once. THWUMMMM. It sounded like a sweet musical note.
[NARRATOR] Before the Suitors could even close their gaping mouths, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) notched a special whistling trick-arrow, pulled it back to his ear, and let it fly.
[NARRATOR] ZIIIIIP!
[NARRATOR] The arrow rocketed straight through the tiny circular holes of all twelve axe handles in a single flash, bounced off a brass shield on the far wall, cut the rope holding up the giant velvet banquet curtains, and dropped the massive, heavy fabric like a giant net right over all one hundred Suitors, tangling them up in a single giant bundle alongside a cascade of dumped flour sacks from the pantry rafters!

[SCENE 16: The Honey-Nut Pancake Reunion]
[NARRATOR] SHIMMER! [Athena](/əθˈiːnə/) waved her hand from the rafters, and the grandpa bathrobe vanished. Standing in the center of the hall in his shining royal tunic was Captain [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/)!
[NARRATOR] Peeking out from the giant net of velvet curtains like powdered-sugar ghosts, the tangled Suitors cried:
[ANTINOUS] The King is back! We're sorry we ate your cheese! Please don't put us in the dungeon!
[NARRATOR] [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) laughed, clapping his hands together as [Telemachus](/təlˈɛməkəs/) stood proudly by his side.
[ODYSSEUS] The dungeon? Worse! You guys have twenty years of dirty dishes to wash, the front lawn needs mowing, and you aren't leaving until the castle floors are sparkling!
[NARRATOR] As the hundred Suitors scrambled toward the kitchen with mops and sponges, Queen [Penelope](/pənˈɛləpi/) walked down the grand staircase. She looked at [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/), smiled, and walked over with a warm plate stacked ten inches high with golden honey-nut pancakes.
[NARRATOR] [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) hugged his wife and his son, tossed a pancake crust to old [Argos](/ˈɑːɹɡOs/), and looked out the window toward the sparkling blue sea. Taking a big bite, [Odysseus](/Odˈɪsiəs/) said:
[ODYSSEUS] You know, rowing around the world and outsmarting [Cyclopses](/sˈIklɑːpsiːz/) and six-headed dragons is fine... but there really is no place like home.
