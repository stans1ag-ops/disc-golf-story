# The Fish That Pulled Back
*A bedtime adventure for Shane and Dad*
*About 2–3 minutes to read aloud*

## Out on the lake

Shane hurried down the dock with his yellow fishing rod. His orange life jacket was buckled tight. “Do you think we’ll see a fish today?” he asked.

“Let’s find out,” said Dad. He helped Shane onto the pontoon and started the motor. The boat hummed across Houghton Lake. Near the shore, tall reeds swayed in the breeze.

Shane pointed to a quiet patch of water. “There. That looks like a good hiding place.” Dad cut the motor. The lake grew still. Shane leaned close and whispered their plan: “Watch the bobber.”

> **Say it with Shane:** Watch the bobber.

## Wait for it

Dad helped Shane cast. The red and white bobber landed with a tiny plop. They watched it float between the reeds.

A leaf drifted past. “Is that a fish?” Shane asked. “Just a leaf,” said Dad. A turtle poked its nose above the water. “How about now?” “That’s our neighbor,” Dad whispered.

Then the bobber dipped. Once. Twice. Down it went! The rod bent in Shane’s hands. Dad put his hands beside Shane’s on the handle. “Ready?” he asked. Shane nodded. Together they reeled.

> **Say it with Shane:** Watch the bobber. Here we go!

## The big splash

Something green burst out of the lake. Splash! Water sprinkled Shane’s cheeks. A bass shook its tail and dropped back beside the boat.

“It pulled back!” Shane laughed. “It sure did,” said Dad. They brought the fish close. Dad held it gently while Shane looked at its bright scales and wide mouth. “Hello, fish,” Shane said.

They let the bass slip back into the lake. For a moment it stayed near the boat. Then its tail flicked, and it vanished under the reeds.

On the ride home, Shane kept one hand on Dad’s arm and one on the yellow rod. “Can we come back?” he asked. “Of course,” said Dad. “Maybe we’ll see another one.”

> **Say it with Shane:** There it goes!
