Chicken Little

A traditional folk tale • Also known as Henny-Penny • A funny bedtime tale about panicking — and checking for acorns.

Scene 1 • Morning in the Rickyard

One day Henny-penny was picking up corn in the rickyard under the big oak tree.

Scene 2 • Whack!

Whack! An acorn hit her upon the head.

🐔 Henny-penny
“Goodness gracious me! The sky's a-going to fall; I must go and tell the King.”
Scene 3 • The Sky's A-Falling!

So she went along, and she went along, and she went along...

🐔 Henny-penny
“The sky's a-falling! The sky's a-falling!”
Scene 4 • Cocky-locky Joins

Till she met Cocky-locky.

🐓 Cocky-locky
“Where are you going, Henny-penny?” — “Oh! I'm going to tell the King the sky's a-falling.” — “May I come with you?” — “Certainly!”
Scene 5 • Ducky-daddles Joins

They went along, and they went along, till they met Ducky-daddles.

🦆 Ducky-daddles
“Oh! we're going to tell the King the sky's a-falling.” — “May I come with you?” — “Certainly!”
Scene 6 • Goosey-poosey Joins

They went along, and they went along, till they met Goosey-poosey.

🪿 Goosey-poosey
“Oh! we're going to tell the King the sky's a-falling.” — “May I come with you?” — “Certainly!”
Scene 7 • Turkey-lurkey Joins

They went along, and they went along, till they met Turkey-lurkey.

🦃 Turkey-lurkey
“May I come with you?” — “Oh, certainly!”
Scene 8 • Foxy-woxy Appears

They went along, and they went along, till they met Foxy-woxy at the edge of the dark wood.

🦊 Foxy-woxy
“Oh! but this is not the way to the King. I know the proper way; shall I show it you?” — “Oh, certainly, Foxy-woxy!”
Scene 9 • The Dark Hole

So they went along, and they went along, till they came to a narrow and dark hole. This was the door of Foxy-woxy's burrow.

🦊 Foxy-woxy
“This is the short cut to the King's palace. Follow me!”
Scene 10 • Into the Burrow

So Foxy-woxy slipped into the dark burrow, and turned round to wait. Now Turkey-lurkey was the first to go through the dark hole into the burrow.

Scene 11 • Hrumph! Hrumph! Hrumph!

He hadn't got far when — “Hrumph!” Foxy-woxy snapped off Turkey-lurkey's head. Then Goosey-poosey went in, and — “Hrumph!” Then Ducky-daddles waddled down, and — “Hrumph!”

Scene 12 • Cock-a-doodle-doo!

Then Cocky-locky strutted down into the burrow. But Cocky-locky will always crow whether you want him to do so or not...

🐓 Cocky-locky
“Cock-a-doodle-doo!”
Scene 13 • Henny-penny Flees

Now when Henny-penny, who had just got into the dark burrow, heard Cocky-locky crow...

🐔 Henny-penny
“My goodness! It must be dawn. Time for me to lay my egg.”
Scene 14 • Safe at Last

So she escaped — but she never told the King the sky was falling!

Scene 15 • Only an Acorn

After all, the sky was never falling at all. It was only ever an acorn. The end.

💭 Bedtime Chat

What should Henny-penny have done before running to tell the King?

🐔 Check First Henny-penny never looked up to see what hit her! When something surprises you, take a breath and check what really happened before you panic.
🦊 Beware of Flattery Foxy-woxy tricked the birds by pretending to help. Be careful of strangers offering shortcuts — and always tell a grown-up where you are going.
